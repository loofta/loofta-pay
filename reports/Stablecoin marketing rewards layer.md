# Lock campaign dollars until work proves

The million-dollar story is not a better crypto giveaway. It is **campaign money that cannot move until the work is proven**: a brand or agency locks digital dollars, Loofta verifies the agreed action, and payout is automatic and auditable. That sentence is what Tremendous did when it rebuilt a consumer gifting toy into bulk business payouts, what Gleam already tells agencies when it says to sell outcomes rather than raffles, and what Circle and Stripe now sell as digital dollars the buyer never has to hold ([Tremendous About](https://www.tremendous.com/about/); [Gleam for agencies](https://gleam.io/solutions/best-giveaway-software-for-agencies); [Circle CPN Managed Payments](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-launches-cpn-managed-payments-a-full-stack-platform-for-seamless-stablecoin-settlement)). At a **5% take, $1M ARR requires about $20M of payout GMV** a year — a number five raid retainers and five raffles a week, currently about **$800/month**, cannot compound into. What has to be true is a usage-based take on money moved through **2–20 payout aggregators** (creator platforms, research ops, KOL desks), chance draws and Megapot tickets parked as optional crypto modules, and a custody design that either never holds USDC or is licensed like Circle and MoonPay. What must not be true is copying Galxe, productizing a raid bot, or asking a CPG lawyer to bless “like and share for a USDC lottery.”

## Escrowed performance marketing is the only sentence that leaves crypto

Companies that crossed the “crypto product” stigma sold a **business money job** — payouts, checkout, global payables, embedded accounts — and hid the chain. Tremendous’s homepage is “Rewards and payouts, simplified,” not gift cards and not crypto; the company claims **25,000+ companies, 100 million+ payouts, $3B+ delivered**, and a VC-free profitable business that started as GiftRocket in 2010 and relaunched for bulk incentives in 2018 ([Tremendous About](https://www.tremendous.com/about/)). Co-founder Nick Baum’s lightbulb was that Nike and SoulCycle were already using a “dinky consumer” gifting site to pay survey participants and run employee recognition; typical jobs became employee recognition, user-research incentives, and **marketing incentives** ([Just Entrepreneurs](https://justentrepreneurs.co.uk/meet-the-founders-interview-series/meet-the-co-founder-of-tremendousc); [Leaders in Payments](https://leadersinpayments.com/2022/09/20/nick-baum-co-founder-and-ceo-of-tremendous-episode-185/)). Pricing is **$0 platform fee**, gift cards at face value, and **4–6% on Venmo/PayPal/Cash App/bank**, with volume talks above **$200,000 sent per year** ([Tremendous Pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/)). Circle’s April 2026 CPN Managed Payments launch sells “regulated digital dollars **without managing digital assets directly**,” citing **$70T cumulative USDC on-chain settlement** as of March 25, 2026 ([Circle CPN Managed Payments](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-launches-cpn-managed-payments-a-full-stack-platform-for-seamless-stablecoin-settlement)). Stripe documents USDC as a payment method that **settles into a Stripe USD balance**; Tempo’s Stripe case study is explicit that a business “does not need to know their payout settled on Tempo” ([Stripe accept stablecoins](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/accept-stablecoin-payments); [Stripe and Tempo](https://tempo.xyz/blog/stripe-and-tempo-stablecoin-settlement-for-global-money-movement/)). USDC as campaign currency is the second sentence, never the first.

The four candidate stories collapse to one. **Anti-fraud giveaway** is table stakes Gleam already owns: platform-verified actions, auditable random winners, auto-removal of duplicate IPs and disposable emails, priced from **$29/month** for Competitions through **$749–$1,199/month** Premium packages ([Gleam](https://gleam.io/); [Gleam package pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing/package)). Gleam’s agency page already says **“Sell outcomes, not just a giveaway”** and maps actions to email, traffic, follows, referrals, and UGC ([Gleam for agencies](https://gleam.io/solutions/best-giveaway-software-for-agencies)). That is a contest OS, not a money rail, and KingSumo’s **$49 lifetime** plugin shows the ceiling of “just a giveaway widget” ([KingSumo pricing](https://kingsumo.com/pricing)). **Global creator payroll** is a real budget that Toku, Tipalti, Deel, and Rise already own: KYC, contracting, recurring payouts, same-day in 100+ countries, with Rise processing **$800M+ payroll of which 60% uses USDC** ([Toku AOR](https://www.toku.com/aor); [Circle × Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise)). Competing there from a giveaway UI is a category error. **USDC as campaign currency** is Circle and Stripe’s rail, not a product. The story that uses Loofta’s actual wedge — escrow, verification, auto-payout — and names a Web2 budget line is **escrowed performance marketing**. ChainReach already writes it as “lock funds in escrow → verify platform/hashtags/timing/engagement → pay only when results are verified,” fiat or USDC ([ChainReach](https://www.chainreach.ai/)). Vaultion sells influencer escrow at **4% down to 2%**, release on delivery ([Vaultion](https://vaultion.org/influencer-escrow)). Loofta’s current public copy (“prize sits in escrow… drawn on-chain… paid straight from escrow”) is the right *mechanism* and the wrong *buyer sentence* if the noun remains “giveaway.”

Chance-based giveaways are the wrong core SKU for non-crypto expansion because they trigger lottery law. The expansion-safe SKU is **guaranteed, verified payout for completed work** — Tremendous incentives, Tipalti creator payouts, ChainReach bounties — not a VRF draw. The raid subscription survives only if it is re-specified as a **bounty subscription**: recurring budget, identity-bound completions, paid when the deliverable is proven. Follow-and-like waves are the product the FTC calls fake indicators of influence.

## Twenty million dollars of GMV sits behind one million of take-rate

Influencer marketing is a **~$24B (2024) / $32.55B (2025)** creator-fee market, not a prize-pool market ([Influencer Marketing Hub, Experts Report](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-experts-report/)). Typical paid programs cluster at **$1k–$5k/month for SMBs** and **$5k–$20k/month mixed mid-tier**, almost all of it creator invoices ([LMG Media](https://lmg.media/how-much-does-influencer-marketing-cost)). Nano and micro rates concentrate **under $500**; UGC is **~80% under $500** ([IMH Benchmark Report 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/)). A 5% take on $20M prize GMV is **~0.06% of that $32.55B** — mathematically easy if Loofta sits in the payout flow, commercially hard because brands already pay creators by invoice, agency, and PayPal. Do not sell TAM as $32B influencer. Prize-pool GMV is the SAM, and it is one to two orders of magnitude smaller.

Web2 contest software is a **small, price-compressed category**. Viral Loops did **CA$1.3M revenue in 2025** at ~86% gross margin and sold for **CA$2.3M** ([EMERGE acquisition](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/emerge-completes-acquisition-of-viral-loops-launching-profitable-b2b-vertical-866545544.html)). Woobox Ultra is **$199/month for 10 live client sites** ([Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing)). Rafflecopter shut down **1 October 2025** after fifteen years ([Gleam Rafflecopter alternatives](https://gleam.io/blog/rafflecopter-alternatives/)). Practitioner prize bands are **$10–$50 SMB Instagram** and **$150–$500 flagship social** ([LikesNetwork](https://likesnetwork.com/blog/how-to-run-an-instagram-giveaway); [Web Tonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/giveaway-ideas)). Influencer giveaway posts all-in run **$525–$3,875 micro** and **$3,150–$13,200 mid-tier**, of which the prize is **$100–$2,000** ([InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/influencer-giveaway-campaign-guide/)). ESL FACEIT’s Gleam case study ran **447+ giveaways** with prizes **supplied by sponsors as hardware and tickets**, not cash rails ([Gleam ESL FACEIT](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study)). You cannot build $1M on Gleam-class prize pools without either becoming the default rail for tens of thousands of campaigns or sitting on **creator cash**, which is influencer spend, not contest spend. Future Market Insights’ **~$127M 2025** online-sweepstakes-platform teaser is the more plausible software order of magnitude than a paywalled $1B “sweepstakes software” number with unpublished methodology ([Future Market Insights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/online-sweepstakes-platform-market)).

Crypto quest reward GMV is the closest analogue and it is **tens of millions, cyclical with TGEs**, not billions. Agency guide budgets for a quest campaign are typically **$1,000–$20,000** all-in; cost per acquired wallet **$0.50–$5** well-designed and **>$20** poorly designed; day-30 retention **below 5% means farmers** ([Suniko](https://suniko.agency/quest-platforms-for-crypto-marketing-galxe-vs-layer3-vs-zealy-a-founders-guide/)). Observed X raid pots are tiny unless bundled into a launch: Zealy’s own published bursts were **$250 / $500 / $525** total at **$10–$100 per qualifying post** ([Zealy, How to Grow Followers on X](https://zealy.io/blog/how-to-get-x-followers-for-a-crypto-project)); memecoin 30-day stacks are **$8k–$25k** typical, with KOLs dwarfing the raid pot ([LASTPROOF](https://lastproof.app/blog/how-much-does-memecoin-marketing-cost)). This SAM can still fund Loofta. It does not get Loofta out of crypto, and it is already being taxed by Galxe seats at **$999–$1,699/month**, Zealy at **$149+/month**, and Layer3’s token stake ([Galxe Business+](https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/11568587-business-overview); [Zealy Pricing](https://zealy.io/pricing)).

The arithmetic that actually hits $1M is concentrated, not hundred-logo PLG. Tremendous needs **$25M cash GMV at 4%**; it treats **~$15k–$17k/month** as the volume-discount *enterprise* threshold, not the SMB default ([Tremendous Pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/)). Forsta, a research platform with **2,500+ customers**, sent **more than $2 million** through Tremendous since 2018 — a platform customer, not 2,500 brand wallets ([Tremendous Forsta](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tremendous-helps-forsta-send-2-million-in-research-incentives-in-100-countries/)). Impact.com paid **$4.5B to partners in 2024** on **$100B GMV** at **$200M ARR**, implying ~**4.4% of partner payments** and ~**0.2% of GMV** — a 5% prize-pool take is rich versus Impact-on-GMV and in-line versus Impact-on-payouts, defensible only if Loofta is the payout rail ([AffRate 2024](https://affrate.com/affiliate-marketing/affiliate-industry-report-2024/)). Linear growth of five token raffles at $20–50k pools, 5% take, six campaigns a year produces **~$30–75k**, not $1M. Eighty-four Galxe Essentials seats at **$11,988/year** also produce $1M, and they are all crypto ([Zealy on Galxe pricing](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)). The path that matches analogs is **hybrid**: agency workspaces in the Gleam Premium band plus **2–5% of $18–35M GMV**, or **2–8 payout-heavy platforms/agencies** at high-five to low-six ACV. One creator marketplace paying **$400k–$800k/month** in earner rewards at 4–5% is **$200k–$480k ARR per logo**. Two to five such logos is the non-crypto $1M. Eight Web3 clipping agencies aggregating **20 clients × $10k/month × 5%** is **~$120k ARR each** and still crypto, but it concentrates GMV the way Gleam packages one login across many independently billed client sites.

## Two to twenty aggregators beat a hundred Shopify brands

The buyers who write checks for *campaign software* are ecommerce, gaming, publishers, and agencies. They mostly fund **product and coupon prizes** and pay **$29–$499/month for SaaS**, not $15k/month in cash GMV ([Gleam pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing); [Viral Loops pricing](https://viral-loops.com/pricing); [Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing)). Gleam case studies — ESL FACEIT, Kinguin, MAINGEAR, She Shopped running sponsor giveaways for **45 businesses** — confirm the prize is in-kind and the software is an entry collector ([Gleam ESL FACEIT](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study); [Gleam She Shopped](https://gleam.io/stories/sheshopped)). What those tools do not do is prize escrow, mass cash payout, KYC, or global disbursement. Viral Loops Growing already wires **Stripe and Tremendous** for automated fiat/gift-card rewarding — the Web2 version of Loofta’s stack, minus crypto ([Viral Loops pricing](https://viral-loops.com/pricing)). Selling this buyer “Gleam but crypto” fails because the marketing manager has no USDC wallet, legal blocks crypto prizes in US/EU sweepstakes, and the prize is a toaster. The wedge on this segment is only **cash/USDC aimed at emerging-market audiences**, or **paying the creators who promote the contest**, not the contestants.

The buyers who already write checks for *cash-like rewards* are research/incentive ops, Web3 growth teams, and creator/gig platforms paying people in corridors where PayPal fails. Meta began paying select Facebook creators in **USDC on Solana or Polygon via Stripe** in Colombia and the Philippines ([CoinDesk, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/tech-giant-meta-starts-paying-some-creators-in-stablecoin-with-stripe-s-support)). Stripe Connect’s current stablecoin-payout recipient list includes PH, CO, AR, PE, MX, TH, MY, KE, GH, ZA and **omits Nigeria, India, and Brazil** ([Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)). A rewards layer that only wraps Stripe misses the corridor where USDC most obviously beats PayPal. The winning UX is **brand funds USD or USDC, participant claims via email or X, local off-ramp to NGN/PHP/MXN**. Wallet-only loses the Gleam customer and many creators. Tremendous still wins US/UK gift-card incentives at **0% software fee**; USDC wins where cash is required, PayPal/Wise fail or take 4–8%, and holding dollars is itself the product.

Agencies are a real **seat buyer and a real channel**, usually not the economic source of prize money. Woobox Ultra is copy-positioned as “scale your client output”; She Shopped ran Gleam for 45 partner businesses; ESL’s prizes came from sponsors ([Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing); [Gleam She Shopped](https://gleam.io/stories/sheshopped); [Gleam ESL FACEIT](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study)). Forsta is the clean analogue on the payout side: **2,500 brand clients never talk to Tremendous**; Forsta sends the $2M ([Tremendous Forsta](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tremendous-helps-forsta-send-2-million-in-research-incentives-in-100-countries/)). Licensed sportsbooks spent **$42M+ in credits and bonuses in Pennsylvania in a single month** and DraftKings booked **$361 million** of Q3 2025 marketing, but the reward is a **bonus bet with playthrough**, not a withdrawable dollar ([InGame](https://www.ingame.com/pennsylvania-new-jersey-september-2025-revenue/); [DraftKings marketing mix](https://dcf-analysis.com/products/dkng-marketing-mix)). Those budgets look rich and are the wrong product.

Distribution that historically worked for contest and payout tools is **agency white-label, Shopify/app-store embed, and usage-based APIs sold to finance/research/HR**, then marketing. Galxe’s Telegram Mini App putting Task-to-Earn in front of **950k users** is the crypto-native channel that traps the company in crypto ([The Block / Galxe](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2025-02-13-how-galxe-is-bringing-smarter-community-building-to-web3-340650)). Do not treat Telegram or X ads as the path out. Sit **on** Circle and Stripe as the fiat edge so the buyer’s CFO never opens a wallet UI.

A realistic 24-month sequence starts where Loofta already converts: **Web3 marketing agencies and KOL desks** that invoice in USDC and currently run raids on spreadsheets (months 0–9). They are still crypto, but they are multipliers. Next, **creator, affiliate, and ambassador platforms** that need an API to lock a budget and release per verified deliverable (months 6–18) — Tremendous’s buyer minus full payroll/EOR. Last, **non-crypto performance agencies and DTC in UK/EU/SEA/LATAM** (months 12–24), only after chance is off by default, FTC/ASA disclosure helpers exist, and fiat-in / USDC-in-escrow / fiat-or-USDC-out is live. Geography: serve crypto-native agencies wherever they already pay USDC, **geo-fence chance promotions from Florida, New York, and US consumers** unless AMOE and bonding exist, keep Megapot out of the US, and take UK/EU Web2 first because ASA explicitly rewards verifiable process and timely award. US Web2 waits on a custody decision.

## Lottery law, fake likes, and unlicensed custody veto the current homepage

Three legal frames kill a US/UK brand rollout, and the current product sentence hits all three. US Postal Inspection Service: a sweepstakes awards prizes by chance with **no purchase or entry fee**; a lottery is the same plus **payment to participate**, illegal except for states and charities ([USPIS Pub 546](https://www.uspis.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/pub-546%5Fconsumers-guide-to-sweepstakes-lotteries%5F508.pdf)). Holland & Knight: illegal lottery = prize + chance + consideration, and **social tasks (like, comment, share) can be consideration**; the conservative fix is an AMOE so “no purchase necessary” ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2022/05/marketers-beware-your-social-media-sweepstakes-or-contests-could-be)). Florida Stat. § 849.094 requires a trust account or surety bond filed **at least seven days before start** for game promotions with announced prizes **above $5,000**, plus a $100 fee; New York files **≥30 days**; nationwide online sweeps must void those states or comply ([Florida Senate 849.094](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/0849.094); [Klein Moynihan](https://kleinmoynihan.com/sweepstakes-registration-and-bonding-requirements/)). Asking a CPG legal team to run “like and share to enter, prize drawn at random, prize is USDC or lottery tickets, funds sit in a crypto escrow” is lottery plus endorsement plus MSB plus gambling in one sentence. The regulatory escape hatch that matches the winning story is **remove chance**. Pay every qualifying verified completion from escrow. FTC and ASA disclosure still apply; lottery registration generally does not.

Paying for social engagement is its own veto. The FTC Endorsement Guides treat a sweepstakes entry or small gift as a **material connection** that must be disclosed; contest hashtags must include “contest” or “sweepstakes,” not `#sweeps` ([FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)). 16 CFR Part 255 Example 13 makes it a deceptive practice to **purchase or create fake indicators of social media influence** and also to **sell or distribute** them ([eCFR 16 CFR 255](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255)). UK ASA: prize promotions on social are CAP Code Section 8; promoters — brand **and** influencer as joint promoters — must administer fairly, use a verifiably random process, and award the prize **within 30 days**; any payment to an influencer makes the post an ad that must be obviously identifiable ([ASA social promotions](https://www.asa.org.uk/general/running-promotions-on-social-platforms.html); [ASA influencer guide 2023](https://www.asa.org.uk/static/b3d29825-db12-44c2-bfcff1b344b564b3/Influencerguidance2023v4-FINAL-DMMCA-Holding1.pdf)). Trustpilot clusters around raid vendors as “fake followers,” “bots,” “engagement vanished” ([CryptoClicks](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/cryptoclicks.io)). A “raid subscription” that scores coordinated like-waves is selling the thing Example 13 names. It survives only as paid, disclosed, identity-bound, quality-filtered completions.

Custodial USDC escrow is a third veto, not a feature for a brand GC. FinCEN FIN-2019-G001: hosted wallet providers that receive, store, and transmit convertible virtual currency for accountholders are **account-based money transmitters** ([FinCEN FIN-2019-G001](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf)). An escrow ruling (FIN-2014-R004) exempts acceptance and transmission that is **only integral to the sale of goods or provision of services other than money transmission**, if the company takes possession to assure both sides and releases only when pre-established conditions are met ([FinCEN escrow ruling](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/administrative-rulings/application-money-services-business-1)). A real-time deposit service that holds buyer funds until the buyer instructs payout, without verifying completion, **is** a money transmitter ([FinCEN real-time settlement ruling](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/administrative-rulings/whether-company-provides-online-real-time)). If Loofta’s escrow is “we hold the prize pool and send it later,” that looks like a hosted wallet. If it is a genuine two-sided arbiter — verify the task, protect brand and creator, release or refund on conditions — the integral-to-service theory is arguable and **state MTL and BitLicense analysis is still required**. Circle and MoonPay advertise full US money-transmitter footprints including New York virtual-currency authorization as the price of holding digital dollars ([Circle CPN](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-launches-cpn-managed-payments-a-full-stack-platform-for-seamless-stablecoin-settlement); [MoonPay Business](https://www.moonpay.com/business)). Tipalti’s July 2026 payee agreement allows fiat-backed stablecoin payouts where enabled, with an explicit design: payer remits fiat, a third party converts and sends, **“Tipalti does not at any time take custody… of any stablecoin”** ([Tipalti Payee Agreement](https://tipalti.com/payee-agreement/)). That is the conservative Web2 pattern. This is not legal advice; it is the reason a brand GC will not sign the current homepage.

Megapot tickets as a core brand import an offshore gambling license into a marketing product. Megapot is a **paid $1 USDC ticket lottery**, Anjouan-licensed, Belize-registered, and **geoblocks the US**; about **77.5%** of ticket sales return as prizes ([Megapot](https://megapot.io/); [Megapot prize structure](https://docs.megapot.io/getting-started/how-to-play/prize-structure)). Even a free-entry sweepstakes whose prize is a wagering instrument is a brand-safety veto for agencies and for Meta/X/Google ads policies. Keep tickets as an optional crypto prize SKU. Never make them the company story.

## Do not copy Galxe, productize raid bots, or become another contest seat

Direct competition for “X/Discord tasks → raffle or leaderboard → USDC” is **Zealy, not Galxe**. Zealy shipped custodial USDC lock-and-auto-payout on **17 September 2025**: funded into a community balance, locked at quest creation, automatically sent to connected wallets; raffle, FCFS, top, and vote; project tokens still manual ([Zealy USDC Rewards changelog](https://zealy.io/changelog/2025-09-17-usdc-rewards); [Zealy Rewards docs](https://zealy.io/docs/quest-properties/rewards)). Zealy Standard is **$149/month** billed annually; X tasks are excluded from Free ([Zealy Pricing](https://zealy.io/pricing)). Galxe remains the distribution brand and prices like enterprise SaaS: Basic still requires a **300 USDT/USDC deposit**, Essentials **$999/month billed annually**, Growth **$1,699**, participant claim fee **$0.08** unless the project sponsors gas, Advanced Sybil Prevention gated to Business+ ([Galxe Business+](https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/11568587-business-overview)). Layer3 automates on-chain token claims behind an unpublished price and a **12-month L3 stake** ([Layer3](https://layer3.xyz/)). No live vendor found sells the full stack as a **campaign take-rate ($15 + 5%) with on-chain escrow**. That slot is still open. It closes if Zealy adds on-chain escrow and a $39 raid SKU. Loofta’s $15 + 5% on a **$1,000 prize is $65 all-in**, which a marketing manager with a one-shot campaign might approve; they will not approve Galxe’s **$11,988/year**. Fight Galxe’s ICP and you lose on distribution. Fight Zealy’s always-on community team on SaaS price and you lose on installed base.

Copying Galxe is a documented dead end even when verification is better, because the *buyer* is still a token team buying vanity. Snag’s industry attack is that “today’s top questing platforms are bot farms” and many quests do not verify completion because teams will not pay for the X API ([Snag](https://www.snagsolutions.io/blog/bot-farms)). Zealy’s own August 2026 engineering post says Zealy **does not verify on-chain activity is authentic**; anti-cheat is identifier reuse ([Zealy: wash volume](https://zealy.io/blog/how-to-drive-on-chain-activity)). Galxe spaces that skip credentials “allow participation from multiple or new accounts… expected behavior, not a bug” ([Galxe bot identification](https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/11827806-galxe-bot-identification-system)). Layer3’s founder criticized Galxe’s protocol-first model for attracting “low-quality, spammy projects” and “lower quality users,” then still sold on-chain user acquisition to L1/L2s ([AlphaPlease](https://www.alphaplease.com/p/inverting-the-traditional-advertising)). Intract is the cleanest corpse: weekly users from **1 million to below 50,000**, then shutdown on **12 January 2026**, after large brands learned they could “build their own front end & get users cheaper” ([Intract LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/intract-io_activity-7490649623930454016-pQOd); [quest.intract.io](https://quest.intract.io/)). If the product is a quest board with a leaderboard, a large brand will DIY. Own **settlement** — escrow, verification, payout, receipts — and be embeddable. Do not build a two-sided quest marketplace.

The rest of the map is saturated in pieces and empty as a bundle. Web2 contest SaaS already owns follow/tweet/join/refer/random-draw at **$13–$99/month** (KickoffLabs, Gleam Pro, ShortStack, Viral Loops) ([KickoffLabs](https://kickofflabs.com/pricing/); [ShortStack](https://www.shortstack.com/pricing/)). Mass payout is Tremendous/Tango/Giftbit at **$0 + 0–6%**, PayPal Payouts API at **$0.25 flat USD**, Stripe Connect at **$2 per active account + 0.25% + $0.25** ([Tremendous Pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/); [PayPal Payouts](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/make-payments/mass-payments); [Stripe Connect pricing](https://stripe.com/connect/pricing)). Loofta’s 5% is in Tremendous’s cash band and worse than PayPal/Stripe for domestic fiat; it is justified only if verification plus escrow plus draw travel with the fee. Against Tremendous gift cards at **0%**, 5% looks expensive unless the recipient wants dollars in a wallet in a corridor PayPal cannot pay. Influencer/UGC platforms (Insense **$400/month plus 7–20%** of creator pay, Aspire/GRIN reported **$12k–$40k/year**) sell paid creators, not prize-funded user tasks ([Billo vs Insense](https://billo.app/blog/billo-vs-insense/)). Shopify loyalty (Smile from **$15/month**, Stamped from **$23**) is points on purchases; it crowds Loofta out of the recurring “rewards” budget line and leaves Loofta in **acquisition/campaign** spend ([Smile.io pricing](https://smile.io/pricing)). Affiliate is a **$13.62B US advertiser-spend** market in 2024 generating **$113B sales**, with Awin taking **20% of validated commissions**; do not try to become Impact ([PMA](https://thepma.org/pma-study-affiliate-marketing-industry-grows-49-8-to-13-63-b-outpacing-e-commerce-growth-by-2x/); [Awin vs Impact](https://elitewealthplan.com/awin-vs-impact/)). The unowned wedge is **on-chain prize escrow + quality-weighted raid/bounty verification + automatic USDC payout + Gleam-like campaign pricing**. “Quests for USDC” is already Zealy’s. Follow/join/like without impression or uniqueness checks becomes a paid wash-trading desk. Zealy’s minimum likes/impressions threshold is the current best published counter ([Zealy alternatives](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)). Relish already pays Web2 creators **~$50–$100** for a receipt-verified local action ([Relish campaigns](https://tryrelish.com/campaigns)); that is the quality bar raids will be compared against.

What would not work, in one pass: PLG aimed at 100 Shopify brands putting **$15k/month cash** into a wallet (their prizes are product, Tremendous treats that volume as enterprise, influencer giveaway prizes top out around $2,000). Staying a Galxe alternative (quest points, XP, leaderboards) keeps ACV in the low SaaS band and the buyer in crypto. Productizing Telegram raid shops at **$80–$300 per vetted raid** imports fake-engagement liability ([BlockAI raid guide](https://www.blockmm.ai/articles/static/crypto-raid-services-2026)). Making Megapot the brand turns every non-crypto sales call into a gambling conversation. Leading with “on-chain VRF” to a CMO who wanted a toaster shipped. Competing with Gleam on contest UX instead of beating Gleam + Tremendous on **speed-to-pay and EM reach**. Building a consumer destination app; MoonPay and Privy win as embeds. Treating Circle’s **$14.7B annualized CPN** or USDC’s **$73–77B circulation** as creator-payout TAM — those are B2B settlement and reserves, and Circle’s Q2 2026 letter itself described a **slowed crypto market** with USDC circulation flattening ([Circle Q2 2026](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)). Underwriting 2026 on 2024–25 TGE quest spend is the same mistake.

Conditions under which $1M is realistic: at least one agency or platform sends **high-six or seven figures a year** through escrow; pricing is **usage-based on money**, not $29/month contests; the noun in the deck is **campaign payouts / escrowed bounties**; chance, raid-bots, and lottery tickets are optional modules; fiat on/off or a licensed partner exists before the first CPG pilot; raids are respecified as disclosed, quality-filtered bounties; custody is either Tipalti-style never-hold or a real MTL path; Nigeria and other Stripe-blind corridors are covered by local off-ramp rather than a wallet QR. Conditions under which it is not: five more token clients, a better draw, and a homepage that still says giveaway.

## Conclusion

The current **~$800/month** is diagnostic, not a scaling bug. It proves that crypto operators will pay for escrow plus verified tasks plus auto-payout, and it proves that the *noun* on the box — giveaway and raid — selects the wrong buyer, the wrong legal frame, and the wrong GMV shape. The business that compounds is closer to **Tremendous with campaign escrow for corridors PayPal and Stripe Connect currently miss** than to Gleam with a USDC toggle. Zealy already shipped the USDC lock; Circle and Stripe are teaching CFOs they never have to hold a token; Intract already demonstrated that a quest front end is not a moat. The remaining wedge is settlement quality — on-chain escrow, campaign-priced take-rate, bounties that pay for proven work rather than manufactured influence — and it is a custody and category decision before it is a feature decision. Ship “Galxe with better draws” and the company stays a crypto SaaS in a consolidating, farmer-saturated SAM. Ship “campaign dollars that cannot move until the work is proven,” sell it through aggregators on a take of money moved, and $1M is a concentrated-sales problem rather than a hundred-brand fantasy.
