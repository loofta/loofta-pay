If you're a creator, a freelancer building an audience, or a small team trying to get more eyes on your project, a giveaway is one of the fastest ways to do it. Loofta lets you run one without touching a spreadsheet, a random number generator you have to trust, or a manual payout at the end.

## What a Loofta giveaway is

You fund a prize pool upfront into escrow, set the rule for entering (for example, a quote-tweet), and open it up. Entrants don't need to trust that you'll actually pay out or that the winner was picked fairly, both of those are handled by the platform, not by you.

The prize itself can be **USDC** or **Megapot lottery tickets** — useful if you'd rather give people a shot at the daily jackpot than a fixed cash amount.

## Setting the rules

When you create a giveaway you choose:

- **How winners are picked** — a random draw, first-come-first-served, or manual selection
- **How many winners** — one or several
- **When entries close**
- **Who's eligible** — you can gate entries by minimum follower count, account age, or require a verified account, to cut down on throwaway accounts entering just for the prize

Every giveaway is reviewed before it opens for entries, so what goes live has already been checked for a funded prize pool and a workable entry rule.

## How winners are actually picked

For random draws, Loofta doesn't pick a winner by hand or with a black-box RNG. The draw runs on **MagicBlock VRF**, on-chain verifiable randomness that anyone can check after the fact — either as a weighted raffle across all entries, or a straight draw of however many winners you set.

## Getting paid out

Payout is automatic once the draw runs. Winners don't wait on you to send funds, and you don't have to track down wallet addresses or handle the transfers yourself, the prize pool you funded upfront settles directly to whoever won.

## Running one for your project

If you're trying to grow a following, launch a token, or just get more people trying out something you built, a giveaway gives people a real reason to engage today instead of "eventually." Fund it, set the entry rule, and Loofta handles the draw and the payout.
