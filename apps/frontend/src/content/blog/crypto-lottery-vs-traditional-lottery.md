State and national lotteries have been around for decades, crypto lotteries are the newer version of the same idea, built on different rails. Here's where they actually differ.

## Access

A traditional lottery ticket usually means a physical retailer, a specific country, and often a specific state or province. A crypto lottery is a smart contract, so anyone with a wallet, anywhere it's legal to play, can buy in without finding a store or being a local resident.

## Transparency

With a state lottery, you're trusting an operator's reporting on prize pools and draw results. With an on-chain lottery, the funds sit in a contract anyone can look at, and the draw outcome is verifiable on the same public ledger. You don't have to take an official's word for it, you can check the numbers directly.

## Draw frequency

Most state lotteries draw once or twice a week. Loofta's version, as an example, draws daily. More frequent draws mean more chances over time, but also mean smaller individual jackpots than the biggest traditional jackpots (a Powerball-sized prize is still rare in crypto lotteries today).

## Fees and payout speed

State lotteries route winnings through a claims process that can take days for verification, and larger prizes often involve taxes withheld at source depending on jurisdiction. On-chain, a payout can settle as soon as the draw resolves, no claims office, no waiting in line. Ticket pricing is also simpler: Loofta's tickets are a flat $1 in USDC, no state-specific pricing tiers.

## What hasn't changed

The odds still favor the house over any individual ticket, that's true of every lottery, crypto or not. And the same responsible-play principle applies either way: it's an entertainment expense, not an investment, and it's worth checking what's legal where you live before you play. Crypto lotteries trade the biggest possible jackpots for more access, more transparency, and faster payouts. Which one's "better" depends on what you're optimizing for. If you want to see the daily-draw version in action, [try Loofta's lottery](https://loofta.xyz/megapot).
