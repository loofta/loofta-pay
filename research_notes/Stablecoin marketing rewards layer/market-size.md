# Stablecoin marketing rewards layer — market size

Research compiled 12 Oct 2026. Prefer 2024–2026 figures. Crypto quest spend is separated from web2 marketing rewards. Every cited finding has a URL. Paywalled market-research houses (Mordor, Verified Market Research, Future Market Insights, Cognitive Market Research) are flagged as secondary/unverified.

**Thesis framing for a $1M/year SaaS taking ~5% of campaign prize pools plus a small per-campaign fee:** influencer marketing is a ~$24–33B *creator-fee* market, not a prize-pool market. The addressable slice for a “stablecoin rewards layer” is the *cash/token prize GMV* inside giveaways, UGC contests, quests, raids, cashback, and micro-creator payouts — which is one to two orders of magnitude smaller, and split between a mature but tiny web2 contest-SaaS stack and a cyclical crypto-quest stack that is consolidating in 2025–2026.

## How large is global influencer marketing spend (2024–2026)? Typical campaign budget ranges for SMBs vs brands vs agencies?

### Takeaway
Influencer Marketing Hub (primary industry survey) puts global influencer marketing at **$24B in 2024** and **$32.55B in 2025** (~36% YoY). The 2026 IMH benchmark does **not** publish a 2026 dollar TAM; it reports aggressive *intent* (87.5% of respondents expect budgets up; 72.2% expect +50%+). Typical *paid* campaign budgets cluster at **$1k–$5k/month for SMBs (nano/micro)**, **$5k–$20k/month for mixed mid-tier programs**, and **$20k–$100k+/month** for brand/macro work — almost all of that is creator fees, not prize pools.

### Cited Findings
- Global influencer marketing estimated at **$24 billion in 2024** and **$32.55 billion in 2025**, vs $1.4B in 2014 and $9.7B in 2020; 2024→2025 growth **+35.63%**; 2014–2025 CAGR **33.11%**. — [Influencer Marketing Hub, Benchmark Report 2025 / Experts Report, dated 3 Mar 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-experts-report/)
- IMH’s earlier “State of Marketing” recap also used **$24B by year-end** vs **$16.4B in 2022**. — [Influencer Marketing Hub, State of Marketing](https://influencermarketinghub.com/state-of-marketing/)
- **Conflict — Mordor Intelligence (paywalled):** influencer marketing **USD 31.07B in 2025**, **USD 40.51B in 2026**, **USD 152.56B by 2031**, **30.36% CAGR 2026–2031**; North America ~**35%** share. Mordor’s FAQ on the same page also swaps 2025/2026 labels, so treat this house’s public teaser as messy. — [Mordor Intelligence, Influencer Marketing Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/influencer-marketing-market)
- IMH 2026 survey (600+ marketers, fielded for 2026 planning, published **4 May 2026**): **87.49%** expect influencer budgets to *increase*; **72.22%** expect **+50% or more**; only **5.55%** expect a decrease. **65.9%** expect payback within 1 month (including **48.4%** within 2 weeks). — [Influencer Marketing Hub, Benchmark Report 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/)
- **Who spends / operating model (2026):** **66.3%** of respondents run influencer programs **entirely in-house**. Most commonly outsourced functions: creator discovery/vetting **19.44%**, content production **15.28%**, talent management / paid amplification / fraud checks **12.5% each**. Reporting/analytics is *least* outsourced (**6.9%**). — [IMH Benchmark Report 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/)
- **Who spends / creator mix (2026):** nano expansion intent **51.43%**; micro **52.83%**; UGC **50.00%**. Macro is roughly net-neutral (~20.6% expand vs ~20.6% contract). Cost-range selections concentrate on nano (**29.54%** of cost selections) and micro (**22.36%**), not macro (**4.35%**). Among those pricing tiers: ~**55%** of nano answers and ~**45.5%** of micro answers are **under $500**; UGC ~**80% under $500**. Mid-tier most often **$2k–$5k** and **$5k–$10k** (each ~22%). — [IMH Benchmark Report 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/)
- **Top 2026 challenge:** rising creator costs **35.4%** of selections; fake/bot followers **56.5%** of reported fraud/quality issues. - Social media as an *ad channel* (not influencer-only): IMH cites **$247.3B global social ad spend in 2024**, **$266.92B in 2025**. This is the parent media market; influencer is a ~12% slice of 2025 social ads if you divide $32.55B / $266.92B. — [IMH Experts Report](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-experts-report/)
- **Secondary aggregator (Talentir, citing IMH + eMarketer + Goldman):** ~86% of US marketers estimated to use influencer marketing in 2025 (eMarketer); creator economy **~$480B by 2027** (Goldman Sachs — **not independently fetched**). Average return **$5.78 per $1** (IMH, widely recycled; treat as IMH marketing figure). — [Talentir, Influencer Marketing Statistics for 2026](https://www.talentir.com/blog/influencer-marketing-statistics-2026)
- **SMB vs brand monthly campaign budgets (agency blog, 2025):** start paid tests at **$1,000–$2,000/month** for 3–5 micro posts; realistic small-brand programs **$1k–$5k/month**; mixed micro/mid **$5k–$20k/month**; macro-led multi-platform **$20k–$100k+/month**. Product-gifting nano tests can start at **$500–$1,000**. - Super Bowl 2026 context: 30-second spots reportedly **$7M–$10M**; Salesforce/MrBeast used as IMH’s “creators as formats” example — not a typical campaign. — [IMH Benchmark Report 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/)

### Inferences
- **TAM (web2 influencer fees) ≈ $24–33B (2024–2025).** This is *not* the TAM for a prize-pool SaaS. Most of that dollar is paid to creators as content fees, managed in-house or via agencies/platforms (Grin, CreatorIQ, Aspire, etc.), not as contest prize pools.
- **SAM for a 5% prize-pool take is a thin slice of influencer:** nano/micro/UGC expansion means *more* small cash or product prizes and affiliate codes, not fewer. IMH’s under-$500 nano/micro pricing is the relevant unit of work for a rewards rail.
- A $1M SaaS at 5% of prize GMV needs **~$20M/year of prize pools** if fee mix is all take-rate, or less if a per-campaign SaaS fee is stacked. $20M is ~**0.06% of IMH’s $32.55B 2025 influencer TAM** — mathematically easy *if* you can sit in the payout flow; commercially hard because brands already pay creators via agency/invoice/PayPal, not via contest software.
- 2026 IMH intent (+50% budgets) is **survey intent, not realized spend**. The 2025 survey already showed a drop in “dedicated budget” incidence (85.8% → 75.6%). Do not annualize 72% “+50%” into a 2026 TAM.

### Gaps
- No 2026 realized global dollar TAM from IMH; Mordor’s 2026 $40.51B is paywalled and internally inconsistent on-page.
- No reliable split of influencer spend into (a) creator fees, (b) product seeding, (c) cash prizes/giveaways, (d) software, (e) agency retainers.
- No primary eMarketer/Insider Intelligence page fetched for the “86% of US marketers” figure — only a secondary recap.
- Goldman Sachs $480B creator-economy 2027 figure not fetched from Goldman primary.

## How large is the giveaway/contest/sweepstakes software market (Gleam, Viral Loops, Woobox, Rafflecopter, KingSumo)? Pricing and typical prize budgets?

### Takeaway
Contest/giveaway *software* is a **small, fragmented SaaS category**, not a billion-dollar prize-pool market. Credible public comps: **Viral Loops CA$1.3M 2025 revenue** (acquired for CA$2.3M in 2026); Gleam Competitions from **$29/mo** (free tier exists); KingSumo **$49 lifetime**; Woobox from **$37/mo**; **Rafflecopter shut down 1 Oct 2025**. Typical *web2 social prize* budgets are **$10–$50 (SMB)** to **$150–$500 (flagship social prize)** — software is $30–$300 for the month of the campaign, prizes are the variable, and neither looks like a $1M take-rate business unless volume is huge.

### Cited Findings
- **Sweepstakes software (broad, paywalled):** Verified Market Research values the market at **USD 1.0 billion in 2024**, **USD 1.8 billion by 2032**, **22.40% CAGR 2025–2032**. Definition includes software to run sweepstakes, contests, giveaways, and promotional campaigns. **Treat as a paywalled teaser; methodology not public.** — [Verified Market Research, Sweepstakes Software Market](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/product/sweepstakes-software-market/)
- **Online sweepstakes platforms (narrower, also paywalled):** Future Market Insights projects **USD 127.1 million in 2025** → **USD 219.2 million by 2035**, **5.6% CAGR**. Application mix: marketing/promotion **58%**; on-premises type **36%**. Named players: Wishpond, Woobox, Votigo, plus many sweepstakes operators that are *not* SaaS tooling. — [Future Market Insights, Online Sweepstakes Platform Market](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/online-sweepstakes-platform-market)
- **Conflict:** VMR $1.0B (2024) vs FMI $127.1M (2025) is a ~8× gap. Likely different scopes (all promotional-game software + services vs hosted online platforms). For a SaaS analogue to Gleam, **FMI’s ~$127M is the more plausible order of magnitude**; VMR may be bundling lottery/sweepstakes *operations*.

Built for referral email-list giveaways, not verified multi-platform actions. — [Gleam, 7 Best Rafflecopter Alternatives 2026](https://gleam.io/blog/rafflecopter-alternatives/); [Cloutstack, Gleam vs KingSumo 2026](https://cloutstack.net/compare/gleam-vs-kingsumo)

- **Typical web2 prize budgets (practitioner blogs, not industry surveys):**
  - SMB Instagram prizes often **$10–$50**; treat all-in cost (prize + shipping + creative + ads + labor) as CPAF math. — [LikesNetwork, How to run an Instagram giveaway](https://likesnetwork.com/blog/how-to-run-an-instagram-giveaway)
  - Flagship social prize **$150–$500**; event swag **$2–$5/unit**; budget **~20% of prize value** for promotion/distribution. — [Web Tonic, 60+ Giveaway Ideas](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/giveaway-ideas)

### Inferences
- **Software SAM is probably $100–300M globally if you believe FMI’s order of magnitude**, not VMR’s $1B. Individual leaders are **sub-$2M revenue** (Viral Loops audited-enough via public acquisition; Gleam likely similar or smaller).
- **Prize GMV behind Gleam-like tools is small per campaign.** If Gleam’s 37,180 campaigns averaged a $200 prize, that is **~$7.4M prize GMV across the dataset, not per year**. Even at $500 average and 10k campaigns/year, prize GMV is **$5M** → 5% take = **$250k**, i.e. you *cannot* build a $1M take-rate business on web2 SMB giveaways alone without (a) much larger brand prizes, (b) a huge shift of influencer cash onto the rail, or (c) stacking a per-campaign fee across tens of thousands of campaigns.
- Pricing architecture of Gleam (month-on/month-off, $29–$119) is the current web2 willingness-to-pay for *software*. A “small per-campaign fee” on top of 5% of a $200 prize ($10) is in the same band as Gleam’s whole month of software — **brands will not pay both unless the rail replaces Gleam and the payout ops**.
- Rafflecopter’s 2025 shutdown + Viral Loops selling as a profitable-but-tiny B2B asset is a **maturity/low-growth signal** for web2 contest SaaS, not a land-grab.

### Gaps
- No audited Gleam revenue; LinkedIn $364k is not a filing.
- No public prize-pool GMV for Gleam, Woobox, KingSumo, or Viral Loops.
- No KingSumo/Woobox customer counts from primary pages (only competitor/comparison blogs).
- VMR vs FMI scope cannot be reconciled without the paid reports.
- No UGC-contest-specific market (e.g. Foap, UserTesting, #hashtag challenges) separated from sweepstakes software.

## How large are affiliate/performance marketing and cashback (Impact, Rakuten, ShareASale, Lootably, Swagbucks)? Take rates?

### Takeaway
Affiliate is a **real, large, growing web2 market**: US advertiser spend **$13.62B in 2024 (PMA)** generating **$113B sales**; eMarketer US spend **$12.42B in 2025** and **$13.81B in 2026**; global “industry value” **~$18.4B in 2025** (Cognitive, paywalled, different definition). **Take rates:** Awin **20% of validated commissions** is the only clean public network fee; Impact’s fee is **negotiated, reportedly lower**, plus **~$500–$1,000/mo** platform (anecdotal). Impact paid **$4.5B to partners in 2024** on **$100B GMV** and **$200M ARR** — implying the *software/network* captures ~**0.2% of GMV / ~4.4% of partner payments** if ARR ≈ network revenue, which is the economic slot a rewards rail would have to steal. **ShareASale shut down 6 Oct 2025** (migrated to Awin). Lootably/Swagbucks: **no reliable 2024–2026 GMV found**.

### Cited Findings

## What does a brand actually spend to run one “community raid / engagement campaign” on X/Instagram/TikTok today (agency fees vs prize pool vs software)?

### Takeaway
There is **no standard rate card**. Observed 2025–2026 bands: **web2 IG giveaway** = **$10–$500 prize + $30–$120 software + optional 15–20% of prize in ads + internal labor**; **crypto X raid** = **$20–$800 prize/coordination** at the cheap end, **$250–$525** for a proof-based creator burst (Zealy’s own campaigns), **$500–$3,000/month** for a raid leader; **memecoin 30-day launch** bundles raids into an **$8k–$25k** stack where KOLs ($8k example) dwarf the prize pot. Agency influencer programs are mostly **in-house (66%)**; when outsourced, agencies are paid for *sourcing/production*, not for holding the prize pool.

## Cross-cutting inferences for a $1M/year “stablecoin rewards layer” (5% of prize pools + small per-campaign fee)

These are inferences from the sections above, not additional facts.

- **Do not sell TAM as $32B influencer.** That number is creator *fees*. Prize-pool GMV is the SAM.
- **Two SAMs, not one:**
  1. **Web2 contest/giveaway prize GMV** — likely **low tens of millions per year** across Gleam-class tools if prizes are $50–$500 and campaigns are in the tens of thousands. Software vendors themselves are **~$0.3–2M revenue**. A 5% take here is a **lifestyle SaaS unless you become the default payout rail for thousands of SMB campaigns**.
  2. **Crypto quest/raid/TGE reward GMV** — **tens of millions per hot year** (Layer3 $35M cumulative rewards, Galxe $5M in one Starboard wave, Intract $10M+ lifetime then death). **This is the only SAM where 5% of pools can produce ~$1M** — and it is **cyclical, farmer-heavy, and already has Galxe/Zealy/Layer3 taking fees in USDC**.
- **Affiliate/cashback is the wrong head-on war** ($13B+ US spend, 20% Awin take, Impact $200M ARR). Use it only as proof that **performance payouts are huge** and that **loyalty/cashback already does 55% of affiliate transactions** — i.e. consumers will take rewards; the gap is **campaign-shaped, multi-winner, stablecoin settlement**, not CPA tracking.
- **Unit economics that fit $1M ARR:**
  - 5% × $20M prize GMV = $1.0M, or
  - 5% × $14M GMV = $700k **plus** ~2,000 campaigns × $150 fee = $300k, or
  - Zealy-style software ($149/mo × ~560 paying workspaces) = $1M **without any prize take** — which is what Zealy already is.
- **2025–2026 direction:** web2 influencer budgets *intend* to rise (IMH 2026) while **quest SaaS is consolidating** (Intract dead, Starboard retired, QuestN stale, Rafflecopter dead). The stablecoin *payout* story (Meta, YouTube, Circle payroll) is **real and 2026-dated** but **unquantified**. The product bet that matches the evidence is **“USDC escrow + payout for campaign-shaped rewards (raids, UGC contests, quests, creator micro-bounties)”**, not “take 5% of influencer marketing.”
- **Who writes the check today:** crypto growth leads and memecoin operators ($1k–$25k campaigns); web2 SMB marketers ($29 software + $50–$500 prize); a minority of brands/agencies running always-on nano/UGC. Enterprise CPG is still on agency + invoice, in-house (66%).
