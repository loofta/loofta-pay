# Who pays for marketing-campaign rewards, and which buyer makes a stablecoin rewards layer a $1M business

## Which 3–5 buyer segments are most plausible, with budget owners, spend, sales motion, and stablecoin fit

### Takeaway
The buyers who already write checks for *campaign software* (Gleam/Viral Loops/Woobox) are ecommerce, gaming, publishers, and agencies — but they mostly fund **product/coupon prizes**, not cash, and pay **$29–$499/month for SaaS**, not $15k/month in prize GMV. The buyers who already write checks for *cash-like rewards* are research/incentive ops (Tremendous), Web3 growth teams (Galxe/Layer3/Zealy), and creator/gig platforms paying people in emerging markets (Meta/Stripe/Airtm/Rise). A $1M ARR stablecoin rewards layer is much more plausible as **2–20 platform or agency logos taking a cut of payout GMV** than as **100 brands running $15k/month cash giveaways**.

### Cited Findings
- Gleam Competitions starts at $29/month; the full-app package is advertised from about $119/month with higher package tiers shown at $299 and $479/month; Gleam says brands can upgrade for promotion months and downgrade after, with no long-term contracts. — [Gleam pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing); [Gleam package pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing/package)
- Viral Loops is $35 / $99 / $159 / $279 per month by unique-participant volume (1k / 5k / 10k / higher), with “Automated Rewarding” on the Growing plan and a custom enterprise tier. — [Viral Loops pricing](https://viral-loops.com/pricing)
- Woobox is $0 / $29 / $79 / $199 / $499 per month; the $199 “Ultra” plan is copy-positioned as “Scale your client output” with 10 live sites, and $499 “Max” as “High-volume enterprise teams” with 20 sites. — [Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing)
- Galxe paid plans start at $999/month billed annually ($11,988/year) or $1,166/month quarterly; Growth is $1,699/month billed annually; a free “Basic” space still requires a 300 USDT/USDC deposit. — [Zealy’s 12 Aug 2026 read of Galxe pricing](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)

### Inferences
- **Five most plausible segments, ranked by “can this be a $1M business *and* get out of ‘just crypto’”:**
  1. **Creator / clipping / KOL marketplaces that already pay many individuals across countries** (budget owner: marketplace COO/finance + head of creator ops). They already have GMV; the pain is PayPal/Wise gaps and 4%+ payout fees. One platform paying ~$400k–$800k/month in creator rewards at a 4–5% take is $200k–$480k ARR per logo. **2–5 logos = $1M.** Sales motion: founder-led / partnership, 1–4 months, must offer email/X claim + local off-ramp, not wallet-only. Stablecoin fit: **high for the recipient**, **medium for the buyer** (buyer wants to fund in fiat/USDC and forget crypto). Closest analogue: Meta+Stripe, Airtm, Tremendous monetary rails.
  2. **Global research / survey / UX-test / rebate platforms (Tremendous white-label competitors or Tremendous customers who hate 4% cash fees in EM).** Budget owner: VP research ops or the brand’s insights lead; Forsta is the pattern (platform is the customer, brand is the end client). Spend is many small payouts, not a few big prizes. Stablecoin fit: **high in NG/PH/ID/TH/LatAm where PayPal is expensive or blocked**; **low in US/UK where gift cards already work at 0% on Tremendous**. Sales: integration + security review, 2–6 months. Path to $1M: one Forsta-scale embed, or 20 mid-size research firms.
  3. **Mid-market marketing / social / influencer agencies running contests for 10–50 brand clients** (budget owner: agency managing director; **prize cash still sits on the brand’s P&L**). They already buy Woobox Ultra ($199/mo, 10 sites) or Gleam. What they lack is escrow, cash payout, KYC, and global rails — Gleam/Woobox stop at entries and a winner list. Stablecoin fit: **only if the prize is cash/USDC going to participants in EM or to creators as appearance fees**; most brand prizes are product. Sales: PLG + agency success story, weeks not quarters. Path to $1M: **weak on SaaS fees alone** (~50 agencies × $200/mo = $120k); **possible if the agency routes prize/creator cash through you** at 5% on even $30k/month blended GMV × 50 agencies ≈ $900k.
  4. **Emerging-market consumer apps / neobanks / super-apps that already pay users in local currency** (budget owner: growth + treasury). They will use USDC **as a rail**, not as a consumer-facing “crypto reward.” Meta choosing PH and Colombia first is the tell. Sales: enterprise, 6–18 months, compliance-heavy. Path to $1M: **one logo** if they outsource payouts; otherwise they build on Stripe/Circle directly and you never see the budget.
  5. **Crypto-project growth teams and Web3 agencies (current Loofta customer).** Budget owner: founder / community lead / growth, paid from token treasury. This is the **easiest $1M** (Layer3 already did $16.6M) and the **wrong narrative** if the goal is to leave “just crypto.” Treat as a cash-flow beachhead, not the story.
- Segments that look rich but are **poor product-market fit for a USDC campaign layer**: US/EU ecommerce (prizes are SKUs/coupons), US sportsbooks (promos are non-withdrawable bonus bets, highly regulated), and global neobanks like Revolut (they already credit the in-app fiat ledger after KYC’d spend — they do not pay strangers USDC for an X follow).
- The **narrative that gets this out of crypto** is not “on-chain giveaways.” It is **“Tremendous for markets PayPal cannot pay, with campaign escrow and anti-fraud attached.”** Circle’s published cases (Airtm, Rise) and Meta’s creator rollout are payroll/creator payouts, not sweepstakes. The campaign-specific wedge (escrow + draw + social-action verification) is what Gleam and Tremendous each only half-do.

### Gaps
- No independent breakdown of Gleam/Viral Loops/Woobox revenue mix by industry (ecommerce vs agencies vs SaaS).
- No public ACV or win-rate for selling cash-prize infrastructure to non-crypto CMOs.
- No primary source on typical monthly **prize cash** (as opposed to software fees or product COGS) for Gleam-class campaigns.
- Stripe’s Connect country list and Stripe Treasury’s longer “Global Payouts with stablecoins” list disagree on some markets (e.g. Nigeria appears in some Treasury lists but not Connect). Treat Connect as the product Meta is using; do not assume NG coverage.

## Who currently buys Gleam, Viral Loops, and Woobox, and what do they need that those tools do not do?

### Takeaway
Buyers are growth marketers, ecommerce brands, gaming/esports companies, publishers/bloggers, and agencies/publishers who run promotions **for sponsors**. The tools sell **entry collection, social actions, referral loops, and compliance copy** — not prize escrow, mass cash payout, KYC, or global disbursement. That gap is already filled in adjacent markets by Tremendous/Tango (payout) and by Galxe/Layer3 (on-chain rewards), not by Gleam.

### Cited Findings
- Gleam case studies on the public success pages include **ESL FACEIT Group** (447+ giveaways over 10 years, 29.5M actions, 1M+ subscribers; prizes **supplied by sponsors** such as Intel, DHL, ZOWIE, Red Bull — hardware, tickets, branded goods, not cash rails) and **Kinguin** (digital games marketplace; 2,223 campaigns over 11 years, ~4/week, seven languages, 16M customers, 21.4% average conversion). — [Gleam ESL FACEIT](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study); [Gleam Kinguin](https://gleam.io/success/kinguin-case-study)
- Gleam **MAINGEAR** (custom PCs) ran 59 campaigns over 9 years with NVIDIA/Capcom; one campaign drove 174,287 actions; the prize is a product bundle sold on MAINGEAR’s own site. — [Gleam MAINGEAR](https://gleam.io/success/maingear-case-study)
- Gleam **She Shopped** is an agency/publisher pattern: Lucy ran sponsor giveaways into a 75k shopper community for **45 businesses**, citing 41,000+ actions, 13,000+ email subscribers, 20,000+ Facebook fans “for brands.” Gleam itself notes it has “a lot of clients that let other businesses leverage their audience.” — [Gleam She Shopped](https://gleam.io/stories/sheshopped)
- Gleam package testimonials on the pricing page include Protean (email list 200 → 7,000), Scrapbook.com, PaleoEpic, The Virtual Savvy, Fuss Free Flavours (bloggers), MaxCDN — i.e. **DTC, content sites, and SaaS**, not banks. — [Gleam package pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing/package)
- Gleam markets automatic official rules, no-purchase-necessary where required, and age/country gating; it describes itself as used by “e-commerce brands, creators, and marketers” and names Razer and Shopify as brands. Plans from free to enterprise; Competitions from $29/month. — [Gleam best giveaway tools 2026](https://gleam.io/blog/best-giveaway-tools/); [Gleam pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing)
- Gleam’s separate **Rewards** app ($49/month on the apps pricing page) is **coupons and gated offers**, not bank/stablecoin disbursement. — [Gleam pricing](https://gleam.io/pricing)
- Viral Loops templates are prelaunch, leaderboard giveaway, tempting giveaway, Shopify referral, newsletter referral, milestone referral, customer referral — i.e. **SaaS waitlists and ecommerce referral**, not prize escrow. Fraud checks and reCAPTCHA are listed as plan features; Power ($279/mo) adds “AI-Powered advance Fraud Analysis” and a dedicated AM. — [Viral Loops pricing](https://viral-loops.com/pricing); [WiserReview Gleam alternatives](https://wiserreview.com/blog/gleam-alternatives/)
- Woobox’s $199 Ultra plan is explicitly “Scale your client output” / 10 live sites / 5 seats — an **agency SKU**. — [Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing)
- Tremendous’s marketing-incentives blog lists sign-up, referral, cashback, loyalty, and event incentives as the cash-payout jobs Gleam-class tools do not own; 53% of surveyed recipients wanted to spend rewards anywhere. — [Tremendous marketing incentives](https://www.tremendous.com/blog/automate-marketing-incentives/)
- Tremendous vs Tango: Tremendous is payout-first (gift cards **and** PayPal/Venmo/ACH); Tango (Blackhawk Network since May 2024) is gift-card-first. — [Tremendous vs Tango](https://www.tremendous.com/compare/tango-card/)
- Tipalti Mass Payments starts at $249/month (AP from $99/month); a third-party estimate of Tipalti Premium is $4,000–$5,000 implementation and $500–$600/month. Stripe Connect (platform-priced) is $2 per active account/month + 0.25% + $0.25 per payout. — [Dots: Stripe Connect vs Tipalti](https://usedots.com/compare/stripe-connect-vs-tipalti/)

### Inferences
- **Who buys:** in-house growth/CRM at ecommerce and gaming; content publishers monetizing via brand-sponsored giveaways; digital agencies managing many small brand sites. Budget owner is usually a **marketing manager or agency account director**, not treasury or compliance. Software is expensed in the marketing-tools line (~$30–$500/mo). Prize COGS is a separate, usually **in-kind** line.
- **What they need that Gleam/Viral Loops/Woobox do not do:**
  - **Payout:** winner gets a widget “you won”; brand still ships a toaster or emails a coupon. Cash/PayPal to 50 countries is Tremendous’s job.
  - **Escrow:** no public Gleam feature holds the prize budget in a wallet until the draw. ESL’s model is the opposite — sponsors donate product.
  - **Anti-fraud beyond bots/duplicate emails:** Viral Loops sells recaptcha + “AI fraud” at $279/mo; Gleam gates by country/age. Neither does on-chain sybil, device graphs at Galxe Passport scale, or payout-time KYC.
  - **KYC / tax:** Tremendous advertises W-9 collection and 1099s; Gleam does not. US cash prizes quickly become a tax product.
  - **Global disbursement:** Forsta needed 10 markets including Indonesia, India, Thailand, the Philippines with local gift cards/PayPal — that is why they bought Tremendous, not Gleam.
- If Loofta sells to this buyer as “Gleam but crypto,” the economic buyer (marketing manager) does not have a USDC wallet, legal will block crypto prizes in US/EU sweepstakes, and the prize is a product. The wedge is **only** (a) cash/USDC prizes aimed at EM audiences, or (b) paying the **creators who promote** the Gleam-style contest, not the contestants.

### Gaps
- No public Gleam/Woobox customer-count or NRR figures.
- No documented US brand using Gleam + Tremendous together as a stack (likely common, unquoted).
- Sweepstakes law by US state (registration, bonding, “no purchase necessary”) was not reviewed in primary legal sources here; Gleam’s marketing claims compliance automation but that is not a legal opinion.

## Who buys Galxe, Zealy, and Layer3 today, and is that budget shrinking as token airdrops cool off?

### Takeaway
The customer is a **crypto protocol or L2 growth/community team** (sometimes a Web3 agency buying on their behalf), paying from token treasury for wallet actions and social tasks. Platform fees are $0–$1,699/month plus a large **reward budget** ($1k–$20k typical campaign; Layer3 has processed $285M+ lifetime). Demand is **not proven dead in 2026**, but quality is rotting (sybil, sub-5% retention), Intract’s quest product shut in January 2026, and Layer3’s own research flags TGE/VC cyclicality. This budget can still fund Loofta; it does not get Loofta out of crypto.

### Cited Findings
- Galxe: Blockworks describes Earndrop (launched Jan 2025) as TGE/airdrop infrastructure using Galxe Passport/Score for sybil filtering, with Solayer as a white-label example. — [Blockworks Intel on Galxe](https://app.blockworks.com/report/galxe-the-web3-growth-engine)
- Galxe self-reports on the order of 21–22 million unique addresses / 4,900–7,770 partners depending on the source and date (unaudited). Zealy ~700k MAU. Layer3 smaller, more on-chain. — [Flexe.io quest platforms 2026](https://flexe.io/blog/crypto-quest-platforms/); [Zealy alternatives post](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)
- Zealy (12 Aug 2026): Galxe Essentials $999/mo annual, Growth $1,699/mo; 300 USDT/USDC deposit on free Basic; $0.08 member claim fee unless project sponsors gas; Intract Quest sunset **12 January 2026** (Intract claimed 12M+ questers, 5,000+ partnerships, $10M+ rewards — unaudited, now historical). — [Zealy best Galxe alternatives](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)
- Layer3: $16.6M 2024 revenue (10× YoY), $20M+ 2025 ARR projection, 500+ protocol clients, 3.2M users, ~300k MAU, $285M+ rewards, 98.6% of reward value from **client budgets not L3 treasury**, ~$40k ARPU. Protocols must stake/burn L3 to post quests. Galxe/Zealy basic campaigns free to launch; project still pays rewards + gas. — [Suniko founder’s guide](https://suniko.agency/quest-platforms-for-crypto-marketing-galxe-vs-layer3-vs-zealy-a-founders-guide/)

### Inferences
- **Budget owner:** Head of Growth / Community / Foundation grants, not a CMO in the Fortune 500 sense. Procurement is Telegram + USDC invoice, not a six-month RFP.
- **Is it shrinking?** Mixed, not a clean “yes”:
  - **Cooling signals:** Intract quest shutdown (Jan 2026); Galxe retired Starboard analytics (3 Aug 2026); public criticism of farmer-heavy campaigns; Layer3 explicitly warns of TGE/VC cyclicality; QuestN’s own marketing counters rendering “0+ Total Users” as of Zealy’s Aug 2026 check.
  - **Not cooling (or still large):** Layer3 $16.6M → $20M ARR projection (Oct 2025); Galxe still selling $999–$1,699/mo seats; Presto and Flexe still describe the category as live in 2026; Suniko still quotes $1k–$20k campaign budgets.
- **Implication for Loofta:** Web3 still pays for **escrow + draw + auto USDC/token payout + anti-sybil**, which Gleam never did. That is a real business (Layer3 is already >$1M many times over) but it is the current “just crypto” box. Diversifying requires a different buyer, not a better raid for the same treasury.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 audited Galxe revenue, and Galxe/Layer3/Zealy user counts are self-reported and conflicting.
- No time series of “average quest campaign budget” that would prove a decline; Suniko’s $1k–$20k is a 2026 range, not a delta vs 2023.
- Presto’s $20M ARR and 5–10% take rate are research inferences, not Layer3 audited filings.
- Alea’s Layer3 note (investor research) was only partially visible; do not treat unquoted Alea claims as facts.

## Would consumer apps, fintechs, neobanks, betting/gaming, or creator agencies pay USDC (or local fiat via off-ramp) to reward users for social actions?

### Takeaway
**Creator platforms already do** (Meta USDC in PH/Colombia via Stripe). **Fintechs/neobanks generally will not** pay USDC for an X follow — they pay in-app fiat after KYC’d card spend. **Betting/gaming have enormous promo budgets** but the reward is a **bonus bet with playthrough**, not a withdrawable dollar, and advertising/bonus rules are tightening. **Creator agencies will pay whatever rail clears in the creator’s country**; USDC/local off-ramp is compelling in NG/PH/LatAm and irrelevant for a US creator who already has ACH.

### Cited Findings
- Meta USDC creator payouts: limited group in Colombia and the Philippines; wallet link; tax docs from Meta and Stripe; Stripe: “Businesses can now send stablecoin payouts directly to customers using Link.” — [CoinDesk 29 Apr 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/tech-giant-meta-starts-paying-some-creators-in-stablecoin-with-stripe-s-support)
- A secondary explainer states Meta’s help page later named Argentina, Colombia, Peru, and the Philippines; X (Twitter) creator payouts are described as US via X Money and everyone else via Stripe **to a bank, with no stablecoin option on X’s help pages**. — [BlockchainTips on Stripe USDC](https://blockchaintips.net/stripe-usdc-payouts-for-creators/)
- Stripe Connect: private preview, US platforms, individuals only, USDC to linked wallet; company payees not supported. — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)
- Revolut referral rewards are paid in the **base currency of the Revolut Personal account**; invitees must make genuine card purchases. Gambling, gift-card, FX, and money-transfer MCCs **do not qualify**. Example prize draw mix: 60% £10 / 26% £25 / 10% £50 / 3% £100 / 1% £200. — [Revolut variable rewards T&Cs](https://www.revolut.com/legal/variable-rewards-referrals/)
- DraftKings referral: $100 in **bonus bets** to both parties, friend deposits ≥$25, cap 20 friends/year ($2,000). Welcome offers are bonus bets with expiry, not cash. Q3 2025 marketing $361M. — [DraftKings marketing mix](https://dcf-analysis.com/products/dkng-marketing-mix)
- FanDuel/DraftKings/Fanatics/BetRivers/Caesars refer-a-friend table: $25–$100 **bonus bets / FanCash / Reward Credits**, not withdrawable USD, with deposit+wager conditions and caps (e.g. FanDuel 5 per 30 days). — [RG.org bonuses Sep 2026](https://rg.org/bonuses)
- PA sportsbooks Sep 2025: $42M+ credits/bonuses in one state-month. Missouri launch: FanDuel+DraftKings expected >$100M combined promotional/marketing hit. — [InGame](https://www.ingame.com/pennsylvania-new-jersey-september-2025-revenue/); [Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/missouri-sportsbooks-to-pump-millions-into-launch-promotions-august-19-2025)
- Spain DGOJ-style limits on bonus offers push operators toward brand spend and away from bonus-arbitrage players. — [iGamingB2B Q2 2025](https://igamingb2b.net/online-gambling-marketing/)
- Gleam ESL/Kinguin/MAINGEAR show **gaming companies already buy giveaway software**, but prizes are hardware, keys, and sponsor SKUs. — [Gleam ESL](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study); [Gleam Kinguin](https://gleam.io/success/kinguin-case-study)
- Viral Nation minimum $250k; influencer giveaway total cost often $525–$13,200 per post, of which prize value is $100–$2,000. — [Viral Nation](https://www.viralnation.com/pricing); [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/influencer-giveaway-campaign-guide/)
- Circle Stablecoin Payouts API is explicitly for paying “vendors, contractors, marketplace sellers, remittance recipients, or end customers,” with Address Book screening and Travel Rule (US: ≥$3,000; SG/FR: all amounts). — [Circle how stablecoin payments work](https://developers.circle.com/cpn/stablecoin-payments/concepts/how-stablecoin-payments-work)

### Inferences
- **Consumer apps:** Will pay USDC only if (1) they already operate a creator/earner marketplace, (2) a large share of earners are in corridors where ACH/PayPal fails, and (3) compliance is someone else’s (Stripe/Circle) problem. Meta is the existence proof. They will **not** buy a “raid/giveaway” product to pay random users for likes; that looks like unlicensed lottery + spam.
- **Fintechs/neobanks:** The growth budget is **referral CAC in ledger credits**, gated on spend. USDC as a consumer-facing reward fights their KYC, card-issuing, and “we are a bank” brand. They might use stablecoins **internally** for treasury; that is Circle’s sale, not Loofta’s.
- **Betting/gaming operators:** Biggest marketing-reward budgets in this research, by an order of magnitude. Almost none of it wants to become withdrawable USDC — the whole point of a bonus bet is that it is **not cash**. Offshore/crypto casinos are a different (high-risk, high-compliance) buyer and stay “just crypto.” Licensed esports/gaming **brands** (ESL, Kinguin) already use Gleam; attach payout only for **creator fees and EM fan rewards**, not for US player bonuses.
- **Creator agencies:** They are a **channel**. The money is the brand’s creator budget. If the agency pays 200 clippers in NG/PH/LatAm, USDC + local off-ramp is a product. If they pay 10 US mid-tiers by ACH, it is not.

### Gaps
- No public conversion metrics for Meta’s USDC creator option (take-up rate vs bank payout).
- No primary source that a non-crypto consumer app has paid users USDC specifically for social follows/raids.
- Betting promotional spend is US-state-regulated and not a proxy for willingness to use stablecoins.
- X/Twitter creator revenue-share terms were only described via a secondary blog, not X’s own help pages in this pass.

## Are marketing agencies that run raids/giveaways for 10–50 clients the real customer?

### Takeaway
Agencies and publishers are a **real buyer of the software seat** (Woobox Ultra, Gleam multi-campaign, She Shopped) and a **real distribution channel**, but they are usually **not the economic source of prize money**. The brand (or sponsor) funds prizes; the agency marks up time. The agency *is* the real customer when it **aggregates creator/clipper payouts** across clients (one AP problem, 10–50 brands). For raid/giveaway **contestants**, the agency will only route money through Loofta if the prize is cash and the agency wants to stop being the PayPal middleman.

### Cited Findings
- Woobox Ultra $199/mo = 10 live sites, 5 seats, “Scale your client output”; Max $499/mo = 20 sites, unlimited seats, “High-volume enterprise teams.” — [Woobox pricing](https://woobox.com/pricing)
- She Shopped ran Gleam giveaways **for 45 partner businesses** off one publisher audience; Gleam acknowledges the “run campaigns for other businesses” pattern. — [Gleam She Shopped](https://gleam.io/stories/sheshopped)
- ESL FACEIT: “all giveaway prizes are supplied by sponsors,” so the **media property is the operator** and the **sponsor is the prize budget**. — [Gleam ESL](https://gleam.io/success/esl-faceit-case-study)
- Suniko positions itself as a Web3 marketing agency teaching founders how to buy Galxe/Layer3/Zealy — agency as **advisor**, project as **payer of rewards**. — [Suniko](https://suniko.agency/quest-platforms-for-crypto-marketing-galxe-vs-layer3-vs-zealy-a-founders-guide/)
- Viral Nation enterprise minimum $250,000; payment Net 30; pass-through creator costs are separate from the agency fee. — [Viral Nation pricing](https://www.viralnation.com/pricing)
- Forsta is the cleanest “agency/platform is the customer” analogue on the **payout** side: Forsta’s 2,500 brand clients never talk to Tremendous; Forsta sends $2M+. — [Tremendous Forsta](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tremendous-helps-forsta-send-2-million-in-research-incentives-in-100-countries/)
- Zealy documents that project-token distribution is **manual after winners are decided** — the exact ops pain an agency with 10 concurrent campaigns would feel. — [Zealy alternatives](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)

### Inferences
- **Two agency types, not one:**
  1. **Promo/contest agencies & publishers** (Gleam/Woobox buyers). Customer for seats; not customer for GMV. Land-and-expand: seat fee then optional prize rail.
  2. **Web3 marketing / KOL / clipping agencies** (10–50 crypto clients, weekly raids, leaderboard payouts). These **are** Loofta’s current motion. They feel payout ops weekly. They do not change the “just crypto” story, but they can concentrate GMV: 20 clients × $10k/month rewards × 5% = $120k ARR per agency; **8 such agencies ≈ $1M**.
- Selling “agency dashboard for 50 brands” is the right **UX**, but contracts should bill **usage on payouts** (brand-funded) plus a **platform fee the agency pays** so the agency is sticky when a brand churns.
- Enterprise influencer agencies (Viral Nation class) will not adopt a crypto-flavored contest tool for US campaigns; mid-market social agencies might if cash prizes + EM creators are in the brief.

### Gaps
- No public rate card that prices “we will run your X raid / giveaway” as a productized agency SKU for non-crypto brands (lots of influencer-giveaway how-tos; few invoices).
- No count of how many Web3 agencies actually operate 10–50 concurrent raid retainers vs. project-by-project.
- Job-post evidence for “social contest manager” roles was not collected in this pass.

## Emerging-markets angle: Nigeria, Philippines, LatAm, SEA — does USDC/local rail beat PayPal/Wise for paying creators and participants?

### Takeaway
Yes, with strong evidence on the **recipient** side and growing evidence on the **payer** side (Meta, Airtm, Rise, Circle APIs). Nigeria is the sharpest PayPal/Wise failure; the Philippines and LatAm are where **big platforms have already chosen USDC**. The catch: Stripe Connect’s current stablecoin-payout country list **omits Nigeria, India, Brazil**, so a rewards layer that only wraps Stripe will miss the corridor Loofta already cares about (NG). Local off-ramp (not “here is a wallet”) is the product.

### Cited Findings
- Nigeria: PayPal described as **send-only since 2014**, still no direct bank withdrawals in 2025 guides; Wise **suspended USD transfers to Nigeria 1 Nov 2022**; GBP→NGN re-enabled Sep 2024 only; Payoneer stacked fees cited up to ~8.5% + $29.95/year. Fintechs (Cenoa et al.) pitch US bank details in / USDC-USDT out. — [Cenoa 2025 Nigeria USD guide](https://www.cenoa.com/blog/how-to-receive-usd-payments-in-nigeria-2025-guide)
- Philippines: Wise ACH receive generally free, ~0.5% convert + $0.50–$2.50 payout to PH banks; PayPal commercial receive ~2.2% + FX 1–2%. Meta USDC payouts live for select PH creators; conversion to pesos is via exchange/wallet. — [VaultLeap PH 2026](https://vaultleap.com/blog/receiving-usd-payments-in-the-philippines-2026); [Polygon Labs / Meta](https://polygon.technology/blog/meta-announces-usdc-creator-payouts-on-polygon)
- LatAm: Meta first-wave countries Colombia (and later Argentina, Peru per secondary); Airtm Circle case study is explicitly Latin America, $900M/year, 160k MAU, 35% savings vs remittance, legacy fees “more than 6%,” settlements in minutes vs up to six days. — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/tech-giant-meta-starts-paying-some-creators-in-stablecoin-with-stripe-s-support); [Circle Airtm](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/airtm)
- SEA in research incentives: Forsta/Tremendous project targeted Indonesia, India, Thailand, the Philippines as “hard-to-reach” with gift cards, prepaid cards, PayPal — i.e. demand for **local redemption**, not for crypto per se. — [Tremendous Forsta](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tremendous-helps-forsta-send-2-million-in-research-incentives-in-100-countries/)
- Stripe Connect stablecoin recipient list includes PH, TH, MY, MX, CO, AR, PE, KE, GH, ZA, etc., and **does not list NG, IN, BR**. — [Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)
- Circle Payouts require recipient screening; SG/FR entities require identity, wallet ownership, and a purpose-of-transfer code on **every** payout (Travel Rule). US entity Travel Rule kicks in at $3,000. — [Circle send a stablecoin payout](https://developers.circle.com/cpn/stablecoin-payments/howtos/send-stablecoin-payout)
- Rise: 53%+ of contractors choose stablecoin; 60% of $800M+ volume on USDC. — [Circle Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise)

### Inferences
- **Need is real for paying people**, not specifically for “marketing campaign contestants.” The documented jobs are: creator earnings (Meta), freelance/gig (Airtm), payroll/contractors (Rise), survey incentives (Tremendous in ID/TH/PH). A marketing-rewards layer wins in EM if it looks like those jobs (many small payouts, local cash-out) rather than like a Gleam widget.
- **Payer vs recipient:** EM recipients want dollars that clear. US/EU brand payers want an invoice in USD and no crypto on the books. The winning UX is **brand funds USD/USDC, participant claims via email/X and off-ramps to NGN/PHP/MXN**. Wallet-only will lose the Gleam customer and many creators.
- **Nigeria is both the best story and the hardest coverage gap** versus Stripe. If Loofta already off-ramps Naira, that is a wedge Stripe Connect does not currently advertise.
- Tremendous still wins US/UK gift-card incentives at **0% software fee**. USDC only wins where (a) cash is required, (b) PayPal/Wise fail or take 4–8%, (c) FX/inflation makes holding dollars valuable.

### Gaps
- No survey of “would a Nigerian participant in a brand giveaway accept USDC” vs bank transfer vs airtime.
- Cenoa/Campus Cybercafe are commercial blogs, not regulators; PayPal’s own Nigeria policy page was not fetched.
- No 2025–2026 Circle or Stripe volume split by corridor for *marketing* payouts (only payroll/creator/remittance cases).
- Philippines GCash/Maya international-USD limitation is from VaultLeap, not from GCash’s own docs in this pass.

## What sales cycle and ACV would it take to reach $1M ARR, and is 100 brands at $15k rewards/month taking 5% realistic?

### Takeaway
The example math is internally consistent but **not realistic for Gleam-class brands**. $1M at 5% take requires **$20M/year reward GMV**. “100 brands × $15k/month” is $18M GMV ≈ $900k ARR — a sensible back-of-envelope, except typical ecommerce/agency contest prizes are **hundreds to a few thousand dollars of product**, not $15k/month of cash. Paths that *do* hit $1M: (A) keep selling to Web3 (Layer3 already did), (B) 2–8 payout-heavy platforms/agencies, (C) SaaS seats alone would need hundreds of Galxe-priced or thousands of Gleam-priced accounts.

### Cited Findings
- Arithmetic: $1,000,000 / 0.05 = **$20,000,000** reward GMV per year. 100 customers × $15,000 × 12 = $18,000,000 GMV → $900,000 at 5%. 112 customers at $15k/month, or 67 at $25k/month, would clear $1M at 5%.
- Gleam/Viral Loops/Woobox **software ACV** ≈ $350–$6,000/year (from $29 to $499/mo list). 1,000 Gleam-like logos at $100/mo = $1.2M ARR **with zero take-rate**, but that is a crowded contest-SaaS market, not a stablecoin layer. — [Gleam](https://gleam.io/pricing); [Viral Loops](https://viral-loops.com/pricing); [Woobox](https://woobox.com/pricing)
- Galxe Essentials ACV = **$11,988/year**; 84 such seats = $1.0M. Growth ACV = $20,388; 49 seats = $1.0M. That is what crypto projects already pay *before* rewards. — [Zealy on Galxe pricing](https://zealy.io/blog/best-galxe-alternatives)
- Layer3: $16.6M revenue, ~500 clients, ~$40k ARPU. At that ACV you need **25 clients** for $1M — and they are all crypto protocols. Implied 5–10% of $285M lifetime rewards is consistent with a take-rate business at scale. — [Presto](https://www.prestolabs.io/research/everyones-chasing-airdrops-layer3-is-selling-the-tools)
- Suniko typical Web3 campaign **$1k–$20k all-in**. Even at the top, 5% of $20k = $1,000 revenue per campaign. You would need **1,000 campaigns/year** at that size for $1M, or fewer larger retainers (Loofta’s weekly raid subscription is closer to Layer3’s recurring ARPU than to a one-shot Gleam week). — [Suniko](https://suniko.agency/quest-platforms-for-crypto-marketing-galxe-vs-layer3-vs-zealy-a-founders-guide/)
- Influencer giveaway prize values **$100–$2,000**; total campaign often **$525–$13,200** including creator fee. $15k/month *cash rewards* is above this entire band. — [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/influencer-giveaway-campaign-guide/)
- Tremendous: 4% on PayPal/Venmo/ACH. To earn $1M at 4% needs **$25M** cash GMV. Volume discount conversations start at **$200k rewards/year** (~$17k/month) — so Tremendous itself treats ~$15k–$17k/month as the **enterprise** threshold, not the SMB default. Forsta’s $2M since 2018 is ~$250–$350k/year GMV for a platform with 2,500 end-clients (tiny per brand). — [Tremendous pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/); [Forsta](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tremendous-helps-forsta-send-2-million-in-research-incentives-in-100-countries/)
- TSI sent **$2.1M in employee rewards since 2023** through Tremendous (HR, not marketing). — [TSI case study](https://www.tremendous.com/case-studies/tsi-employee-recognition/)
- Viral Nation deals start at $250k; sportsbook promo budgets are tens of millions per state-month — but those buyers are not shopping for USDC contest rails. — [Viral Nation](https://www.viralnation.com/pricing); [InGame PA](https://www.ingame.com/pennsylvania-new-jersey-september-2025-revenue/)
- Stripe Connect onboarding for stablecoin payouts: request preview, ~two business days to hear back, then due-diligence questionnaire and approval — i.e. **sales-assisted, compliance-gated**, not self-serve for the platform. Tipalti-class AP is $249+/mo plus implementation. — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts); [Dots comparison](https://usedots.com/compare/stripe-connect-vs-tipalti/)

### Inferences
- **Realism by segment for the “100 × $15k × 5%” plan:**

  | Segment | Typical monthly *cash-like* rewards | Sales cycle (inferred) | 100 logos at $15k/mo? | Better $1M path |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Ecommerce / SaaS (Gleam buyers) | Product/coupon; cash rare | Self-serve, days–weeks | **No** | Do not use take-rate; would need 1,000 SaaS seats |
  | Promo agencies (Woobox Ultra) | Brand-funded, usually in-kind | Weeks | **No** at $15k cash | Yes if they **route creator payouts**; ~50 agencies × $30k GMV × 5% |
  | Web3 projects (Galxe/Layer3) | $1k–$20k per campaign; some always-on | Days–weeks, USDC invoice | **Possible** among crypto, not “out of crypto” | 25 Layer3-like logos or 84 Galxe seats |
  | Web3 / clipping agencies | Aggregated client treasuries | Weeks | **Yes as 8–20 agencies**, not 100 brands | Highest concentration; still crypto |
  | Research/incentive platforms | Many $5–$50 payouts; $200k+/yr is “enterprise” for Tremendous | 2–6 months, security | **No** 100 brands; **yes** 1–5 platforms | White-label Tremendous + EM rails |
  | Creator platforms (Meta-class) | Recurring earner payroll | 6–18 months | **1–5 logos** | Best non-crypto GMV; compete with Stripe |
  | Fintechs / neobanks | In-app fiat referrals | 6–18 months | Unlikely to pay Loofta | They build in-house |
  | Licensed betting | $millions, not withdrawable | 12+ months, licensing | Wrong product | Skip for USDC rewards |

- **Sales motion that matches $1M:**
  - *Do not* run a PLG motion aimed at 100 Shopify brands for cash giveaways.
  - *Do* run a **high-touch motion to 10–30 payout aggregators** (creator marketplaces, research platforms, Web3 agencies) at **$30k–$100k ACV** (mix of SaaS + take-rate).
  - *Keep* the current crypto raid/giveaway product as a **cash-flow beachhead** (the only segment already comfortable with USDC escrow + on-chain draw).
- **Pricing implication:** 5% is in-market (Tremendous 4% cash; Presto’s 5–10% Layer3 inference). Competing with Tremendous’s **$0** gift-card list price means you only win the **cash/EM** slice. Competing with Gleam’s $29/mo means you should not expect Gleam buyers to suddenly put $15k/month into a wallet.
- **Cycle time:** PLG contest SaaS = days; crypto growth = weeks; payout-platform embed = months; Meta/Stripe-class consumer app = quarters to a year. A $1M year from **new** non-crypto logos is only plausible if 2026 pipeline is already platform-shaped, not brand-shaped.

### Gaps
- No Loofta current ACV/logo count in this research (out of scope / not public).
- No published close rates for selling crypto rails to non-crypto CMOs.
- Tremendous does not publish average rewards volume per marketing customer, only the $200k volume-discount cutoff and selected case studies.
- Agency “10–50 clients” is a hypothesis from the brief, not a census.
