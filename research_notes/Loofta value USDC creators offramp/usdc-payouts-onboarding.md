# USDC payouts plus consumer off-ramp vs crypto onboarding 2025-2026

Facts as of 2025–2026. USDC on-chain settlement volume and circulating supply are not treated as payout TAM. Recipient demand, corridor failures, and hold-vs-cash-out behavior are.

## Who actually wants to receive USDC versus fiat today

### Takeaway
Demand to *hold* USDC is concentrated in emerging-market contractors, remittance recipients, and creators in inflation/capital-control corridors (Nigeria, Argentina, Philippines, Colombia, Venezuela, parts of LatAm). In the US and EU, recipients want dollars in Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or a bank; USDC is only interesting as a cheaper invisible rail. There is no public evidence of a large US consumer segment that prefers a USDC wallet over Venmo when both are offered.

Numbers are Rise-provided, published by Circle on 2026-09-02. — [Circle × Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise)

### Inferences
- **Two different “receive USDC” jobs:** (1) *dollar savings* in EM (Rise 53%, Airtm hold-until-spend, Argentina USDT savings, Nigeria P2P #1) — here the recipient *wants* the stablecoin; (2) *cheaper disbursement* in US/EU/creator platforms (YouTube PYUSD, Stripe Connect, Meta) — here the sender wants the rail and the recipient usually wants local cash. Loofta’s current crypto-native users look like (1); a US Venmo recipient looks like (2).
- **Creators:** Meta’s country pick (PH, CO, then AR, PE) is the tell. They did not launch USDC payouts in the US, UK, or Germany, where PayPal/Wise already work. Creator USDC demand is a *corridor* story, not a creator-identity story.
- **Contractors / payroll:** Rise is the cleanest named evidence that, given a real choice, a majority of *global* contractors on that platform pick stablecoin. Mix numbers moved (45%→60% volume; 50%→53%+ of contractors); even the conservative figure is large. This is not US W-2 payroll.
- **Remittances:** World Bank consumer remittance fees (US→NG 2.72% advertised average for $200 — see corridors section) understate the *business-to-contractor* problem Airtm cites (6%+, 6 days). The recipient who wants USDC is often a freelancer/creator being paid by a US platform, not a family receiving Western Union.
- **Gaming / marketplaces:** no primary 2025–2026 figure was found for “% of marketplace sellers who choose USDC vs bank.” Stripe Connect’s design (platform stays in fiat; recipient optionally links a wallet) implies the platform buyer does not want to think about USDC. Treat marketplace demand as sender-side rail demand until a Connect adoption stat exists.
- **US/EU consumers who “just want Venmo”** are the default. PYUSD’s 4% hold reward is PayPal trying to *create* hold demand inside an existing fiat account. That is the opposite of onboarding a first wallet.

### Gaps
- No public split of Rise’s 53% by country (how much is US vs NG/AR/PH). Without that, 53% cannot be applied to a US-heavy recipient mix.
- No public Meta take-up rate (% of eligible PH/CO creators who chose USDC vs local bank/GCash).
- No 2025–2026 survey of US creators choosing USDC vs Venmo when both are offered.
- No named GMV for USDC payouts on Stripe Connect (private preview; no volume disclosed).
- Gaming/marketplace seller preference for USDC vs fiat: not found in primary sources.
- Tipalti USDC/stablecoin payout product: no primary 2025–2026 page found in this pass.

## What happens when the product includes a good off-ramp: does USDC stay visible or become an invisible rail?

### Takeaway
It splits by corridor. In the US/EU, a good off-ramp makes USDC disappear (Coinbase 1:1, PayPal 1:1 PYUSD, Stripe “platform stays in fiat”). In EM inflation/capital-control markets, a large share of recipients *keep* the dollar until they need to spend, then cash out locally the same day (Airtm, Rise, Argentina USDT). Building the off-ramp does not kill USDC demand in those corridors; it is what makes the payout usable. It *does* kill “learn crypto” as the consumer story.

### Cited Findings
- Circle/Airtm product design is explicit: land in **USDC in minutes**, **hold as digital dollars**, **convert to local fiat the same day** via cashiers / bank / prepaid card. USDC is both savings instrument and rail. — [Circle × Airtm](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/airtm)
- Rise spent “months fine-tuning” so “from the perspective of the HR manager, paying in USDC is as simple as using any other currency.” Contractors still *opt in* to stablecoin withdrawals at 53%+. Abstraction for the *payer*; choice for the *payee*. — [Circle × Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise)
- Stripe Connect: **“Your platform balance stays in fiat currency, while Stripe handles conversion and payout.”** Recipient links a wallet, sees a USDC balance, funds pay out to that wallet instead of a bank. Transfers in USD “automatically convert to the recipients’ preferred currency.” USDC is a *recipient preference*, invisible to the platform. Private preview, US platforms only. — [Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)
- Circle Payments Network **Fiat Payouts**: OFI sends USDC on-chain; **BFI delivers local fiat** (example: BRL to receiver’s bank). CPN **Managed Payments** is “operate fully in fiat while Circle powers stablecoin payments behind the scenes.” As of 31 Mar 2026, 20+ chains. This is the fully invisible-rail design. — [Circle CPN Fiat Payouts](https://developers.circle.com/cpn/fiat-payouts); [CPN overview](https://developers.circle.com/cpn); [CPN Managed Payments](https://www.circle.com/cpn/managed-payments)
- Meta’s pilot is the opposite of invisible: creators must pick Solana or Polygon, paste a USDC address, and **Meta does not convert to local currency**. Help copy lists GCash, Coins.ph, Bitso, Phantom. The missing piece Meta outsourced to the user is the off-ramp. — [Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1141348158001625); [CoinDesk, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/tech-giant-meta-starts-paying-some-creators-in-stablecoin-with-stripe-s-support)
- US consumer cash-out: practitioner comparison says **Coinbase remains the lowest-friction US off-ramp because of 1:1 free USDC conversion**; Kraken for non-US; widgets for wallets are MoonPay / Transak / Ramp; B2B is Bridge (Stripe) and BVNK. — [Eco stablecoin offramps 2026](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210579-best-stablecoin-offramps-2026-cash-out-routes-compared)
- PayPal: buy/sell PYUSD **1:1, no fee** inside PayPal. Holders who want “dollars” never leave the app. — [PayPal PYUSD](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/manage-money/crypto/pyusd)
- Chainalysis: global **stablecoin balances held $98–109B** through the 2026 drawdown while other crypto balances fell 55.6%; stablecoins reached **22.5% of global on-chain balances** by June 2026. LatAm personal wallet balances **17.6% stablecoins**. People in those markets *do* hold, not only transact. — [Chainalysis 2026 index](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/); [Chainalysis 2026 LATAM](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/latin-america-crypto-adoption-2026/)
- CSAO: **cross-border stablecoin activity exceeded domestic** in every market analyzed (regional ratio **3.2:1**; Malaysia **29.5:1**). Malaysian exchange CEO: “Stablecoins have a relatively weak value proposition for everyday domestic payments. Where the opportunity becomes much more interesting is cross-border settlement.” Countries with working domestic rails (PIX, GCash, UPI) use stablecoins as a *bridge*, then exit. — [Chainalysis 2026 CSAO](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/central-southeast-asia-oceania-crypto-adoption-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Off-ramp quality changes the UX, not the underlying demand split.** US recipient + good off-ramp → USDC is a 30-second gas fee on the way to Venmo/bank. EM recipient + good off-ramp → they keep USDC as dollar savings and drip out to NGN/ARS/PHP when they need cash. Airtm’s whole product is that loop.
- **If Loofta builds Venmo/Cash App/Revolut/Zelle/PAJ Cash cash-out, US/EU volume will look like Tremendous/PayPal** (fiat in, fiat out, USDC as plumbing). EM volume will look like Airtm/Rise (USDC as the balance people actually hold). Pricing, compliance, and support must not assume everyone cashes out.
- **Meta’s gap is Loofta-shaped only if Loofta is the claim + local cash-out**, not if Loofta is “another wallet.” Meta already lists Phantom, Bitso, GCash. The unpaid job is “I got USDC in my email/claim link and I want pesos/naira/PHP today without knowing Solana.”
- **Invisible-rail is what Circle CPN Managed Payments and Stripe Connect sell to platforms.** Visible-dollar is what recipients in AR/NG/VE buy. Loofta cannot win both with one story.

### Gaps
- No public % of Airtm volume that is held >7 days vs cashed out same day.
- No public % of Meta USDC payouts that are immediately sold on Binance/GCash vs held.
- No Loofta-comparable claim-link conversion: email → first wallet → hold vs cash-out, with numbers.

## Named-company evidence: Circle, Stripe, Meta, Rise, Airtm, PayPal, Coinbase, Bitso

### Takeaway
Every large 2025–2026 proof point is **payroll, creator disbursement, or EM dollar access** — not “onboard to crypto.” The companies that scaled it either *are* the local off-ramp (Airtm, Bitso, Lemon) or *rent* Stripe/Circle (Meta, Rise). None of the named successes is a standalone US consumer wallet-onboarding company.

### Cited Findings
- **Circle / CPN:** network for banks/PSPs/fintechs to accept, convert, move, settle USDC; Fiat Payouts = USDC in, local fiat out via BFI partners; Stablecoin Payouts = send USDC/EURC to registered wallets; Managed Payments = fiat UX, Circle operates the coins. 20+ chains as of 31 Mar 2026. — [CPN docs](https://developers.circle.com/cpn)
- **Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts:** private preview, **US-based platforms only**, USDC (and OUSD; Tempo uses USDC.e), **individuals/sole proprietors only** (not companies/nonprofits), Express Dashboard required, Transfers API. Recipient countries include PH, CO, AR, PE, MX, KE, GH, ZA, TH, MY, CL, plus many others; **Nigeria, India, Brazil, UK, Germany, Spain, Italy are not on the published Connect list**. - **Stripe Global Payouts** (different product) lists USDC/OUSD crypto-wallet payouts as **public preview to recipients with a crypto wallet “anywhere in the world”** (NY businesses waitlist). Do not conflate with Connect’s country list. — [Stripe recipient requirements](https://docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/recipient-requirements); [Stripe payout methods](https://docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/payout-methods)
- **Stripe acquired Bridge** for **$1.1B** (announced Oct 2024, closed Feb 2025), Stripe’s largest acquisition. - **PayPal PYUSD:** 1:1 USD inside PayPal; 4% hold rewards; ~70-market expansion claimed; YouTube US creator opt-in via PayPal enterprise payouts (Fortune via secondary). PayPal **Pay with Crypto** for US merchants, global buyers, settlement in local currency, refunds in PYUSD. — [PayPal PYUSD](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/manage-money/crypto/pyusd); [PayPal crypto developer](https://developer.paypal.com/crypto)

### Inferences
- The winning pattern is **local licensed cash-out + dollar balance**, not a global USDC brand. Airtm, Bitso, Lemon, Nubank, GCash, PAJ Cash are corridor companies. Stripe/Circle are the wholesale rail those companies (or Meta) sit on.
- **Meta is not a Loofta analogue.** Meta already has the creators and the KYC’d payout account; it added USDC as an *option* in four EM countries via Stripe. Loofta would be trying to be both the claim surface *and* the off-ramp for senders who are not Meta.
- **Circle’s published cases are payroll and remittance-like payouts (Airtm, Rise, Zebec, BCRemit, Félix, Thunes, LuLu), not consumer wallet onboarding.** That is the category Circle wants USDC to own.

## Which corridors still fail for PayPal, Wise, and Stripe — and is that the real niche?

### Takeaway
Yes: the durable recipient-side niche is **paying individuals in corridors where PayPal/Wise/Stripe bank payouts are missing, send-only, preview-only, or expensive**, not “USDC vs Venmo in California.” Nigeria (P2P #1 globally, PayPal receive only just returned via Paga in Jan 2026, **absent from Stripe Connect**), Pakistan (no PayPal), Ghana (PayPal still out as of Aug 2025), India (PayPal receive-only / no domestic; Stripe Preview; **not on Connect stablecoin list**), and Brazil (**not on Connect list** despite PIX and Nubank) are the load-bearing examples. US/UK/DE/ES/IT are PayPal-native; Stripe Connect also omits UK/DE/ES/IT for stablecoin, which is a product constraint, not proof of recipient demand.

### Cited Findings
- **Stripe Connect stablecoin recipient list (fetched Oct 2026) includes** AR, CL, CO, MX, PE, PH, PY, UY, GH, KE, ZA, TH, MY, US, CA, AU, SG, FR, NL, AT, BE, and others. **Does not include NG, IN, BR, GB, DE, ES, IT, ID, VN, PK, BD.** Individuals only; no companies. US platforms only; NY/HI excluded. — [Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)
- **PayPal Nigeria:** ~2004 send-only / cannot receive; **Jan 2026 return via Paga** — link PayPal to Paga wallet, view balance, convert and withdraw Naira. Not a native PayPal bank payout; 13 years of receive-block. Boycott/skepticism coverage the same week. — [Nairametrics, 27 Jan 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/01/27/paypal-return-sparks-boycott-calls-and-mixed-reactions-in-nigeria/); [The Papers Nigeria, 27 Jan 2026](https://thepapers.ng/2026/01/27/paypal-returns-to-nigeria-13-years-after/)
- **PayPal Ghana:** as of 1 Aug 2025 the communications minister said PayPal issued a **compliance roadmap** for whitelist reinstatement; **not restored** in that report. — [GBC Ghana, Aug 2025](https://www.gbcghanaonline.com/general-news/ghana-pushes-for-paypal-reinstatement-outlines-path-to-compliance/2025/)
- **PayPal Pakistan:** country is not on PayPal’s Asia selector as of **9 Sep 2026**; cannot open a resident account or withdraw to a Pakistani bank. Payoneer also excludes Pakistan-registered accounts from PayPal-funded payment requests. — [ElevatePay, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.elevatepay.co/blog/paypal-in-pakistan)
- **PayPal India (2025):** receive international payments and withdraw to Indian bank; **cannot send abroad, no domestic PayPal** since 2021 (RBI). — [Karbon Card](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/will-paypal-work-in-india)
- **Wise:** India and Nigeria **send-only** — can register and send, **cannot hold balances or get account details** because those countries are not on Wise’s residence list (checked 14 Sep 2026). Argentina: Wise Business yes; **local Stripe: no**. India local Stripe: **Preview**. Nigeria local Stripe: **via Paystack**. — [Cheq Capital 49-country table, 14 Sep 2026](https://cheqcapital.com/en/countries-accepted-by-mercury-relay-wise-stripe/)
- **Global Solo 2026 payment-availability index:** Nigeria **PayPal not on published list**; India PayPal = receive and withdraw; Brazil PayPal fully localized; Argentina PayPal yes; Pakistan/Bangladesh/Egypt/Sri Lanka/Nepal/Ghana/Lebanon/Ethiopia called out as countries people assume PayPal covers and it does not. — [Global Solo](https://www.globalsolo.global/data/payment-availability-index)
- **World Bank RPW Q3 2025** (data Aug 2025, report dated for Sep 2025): **global average remittance cost 6.36%**; **Sub-Saharan Africa 8.46%** (most expensive region); banks 14.99%; digital remittances index 4.59%. US→Nigeria $200: **total average 2.72%** (fee $4.47 + 0.48% FX). US→Philippines $200: **4.52%** (fee $6.23 + 1.40% FX). These are *consumer remittance* mystery-shop prices, not B2B contractor/PayPal payout fees. — [World Bank RPW homepage](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/); [US→NG](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/United-States/Nigeria); [US→PH](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/US/PH); [RPW Q3 2025 PDF](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/RPW_main_report_and_annex_Q325.pdf)
- Airtm/Circle: business-to-worker fees on legacy rails **average more than 6%**, up to **six days**. Bridge/Airtm: **~20% savings vs PayPal or Wise**. Tremendous cash: **4% or 6%**. Rise: wires **$15–50 + 2–4% FX**. The “PayPal/Wise tax” for platforms paying many individuals is 4–6%+, which is the take a payout layer can undercut. — [Circle × Airtm](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/airtm); [Bridge × Airtm](https://www.bridge.xyz/blog/airtm-case-study); [Tremendous pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/); [Circle × Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise)
- Chainalysis 2026: **Nigeria #1 P2P and #1 cross-border**; Brazil #1 overall adoption but **Stripe Connect does not list BR**; PH remittances 5–10% already on stablecoins while Meta launched USDC there. — [Chainalysis 2026 index](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)

### Inferences
- **The niche is not “USDC payouts.” It is “pay an individual in NG/PK/GH/AR/VE/PH when the sender is a US platform and PayPal/Wise/Connect cannot complete a cheap bank payout.”** Loofta’s PAJ Cash Nigeria off-ramp maps onto the single highest-P2P country on earth, which Stripe Connect still does not list.
- **Do not use World Bank 2.72% US→NG as the competitor price for creator/contractor payouts.** Western Union/MoneyGram $200 consumer quotes are a different product from PayPal/Wise/Stripe mass payouts to freelancers. Airtm’s 6% and Tremendous’s 4–6% are the right comps.
- **India and Brazil are traps.** Huge crypto economies (IN #6 2026, BR #1) but heavy local rails (UPI, PIX) and tightening VASP/eFX rules. Recipients often want INR/BRL in-bank, not a USDC balance. USDC is a *bridge* there; local licensed partners (already Bitso, Mercado Bitcoin, Nubank) own the last mile.
- **PayPal’s Jan 2026 Nigeria return via Paga shrinks but does not close the gap.** Receive-into-Paga is still a local-fintech partnership, not “PayPal works like the US.” Fraud history and frozen-funds reputation remain.
- **Stripe Connect omitting NG/IN/BR** is the most important product fact for Loofta-vs-Stripe. A US marketplace using Connect cannot pay a Nigerian creator in USDC through that preview. That is a real hole, not a marketing hole. It may close; it had not as of the fetched docs.

### Gaps
- Wise and PayPal *payout* fee schedules for PH/CO/AR contractors in 2026 (not consumer remittance).
- Whether Stripe Connect country list will add NG/BR/IN in 2026 (no public roadmap found).
- India inbound USDC-to-INR regulatory status post-VASP rules — not reviewed in primary RBI/FIU documents this pass.
- Brazil BCB Resolution 561 Oct 2026 restrictions on eFX-provider stablecoin settlement: cited by Eco, not read in the BCB primary text.

## Is “onboard people to crypto” (first wallet via a claim) still a funded standalone thesis in 2026?

### Takeaway
**Standalone “onboard the world to crypto” is dead as a company thesis.** The 2025–2026 outcome is **acquisition into rails** (Privy→Stripe, Dynamic→Fireblocks) plus **wallets that already have users** (Phantom $3B, 17M MAU). First-wallet-via-airdrop/claim has brutal CAC once farmers are stripped out (Arbitrum ~$2,175 acquired / ~$5,437 retained). Embedded wallets remain a *feature* of apps that have a non-crypto job (payouts, games, fintech). They are not a fundable consumer destination.

### Cited Findings
- **Privy:** $15M (Mar 2025, Ribbit; total >$40M; Sequoia, Paradigm, Coinbase in the round) then **acquired by Stripe 11 Jun 2025**. Continues as an independent product inside Stripe. Privy’s own 2026 onramp post: Stripe Crypto Onramp for US/EU, aggregator for 100+ other countries; “Creating a wallet is only part of onboarding. Users still need assets.” Destination assets include USDC, USDT, Phantom Cash. — [The Block, 19 Mar 2025](https://www.theblock.co/news/deals/2025-03-19-privy-raises-15-million-usd-round-led-by-ribbit-capital-investment-in-web3-wallet-infrastructure-heats-up-347110); [Privy × Stripe](https://blog.privy.io/blog/announcing-our-acquisition-by-stripe); [Privy global fiat onramps](https://blog.privy.io/blog/introducing-global-fiat-onramps)
- **Dynamic Labs:** $13.5M Series A Dec 2023 (a16z crypto, Founders Fund); **acquired by Fireblocks Oct 2025** (secondary tracker; not a Fireblocks primary post in this pass). Total disclosed equity ~$21M. — [Dynamic Series A](https://www.dynamic.xyz/blog/series-a); [Venture Capital Tracker, updated 25 Jul 2026](https://venturecapitaltracker.com/startup/dynamic)
- **Phantom:** MoonPay’s explainer states Phantom raised a **$150M Series C at $3B in January 2025** and reported **~17 million MAU at 2025 peak**. That is a *wallet destination*, not a first-claim onboarding startup. — [MoonPay on Phantom](https://www.moonpay.com/learn/cryptocurrency/what-is-phantom-wallet)

Consumer app + B2B ramps. — [Spark ramp landscape, 8 May 2026](https://www.spark.money/research/crypto-on-off-ramp-market-landscape); [Transak 2026 comparison](https://transak.com/blog/best-white-label-crypto-on-off-ramp-providers)

- **Banxa** acquired by OSL for **CAD $85.2M, Jan 2026**. Ramp market consolidating into licensed incumbents, not new onboarding brands. — [Spark, 8 May 2026](https://www.spark.money/research/crypto-on-off-ramp-market-landscape)
- **Airdrop CAC:** Arbitrum incentive campaign estimated **$2,175 per acquired user** and **$5,437 per retained recipient** after ~60% did not retain (Dune-based, author’s arithmetic). ### Inferences
- **VCs did not stop funding wallets; they stopped funding “onboarding as the product.”** Capital went to (a) infra that Stripe/Fireblocks needed to buy, (b) Phantom-scale consumer wallets, (c) MoonPay-scale ramps. A claim-to-first-wallet consumer app is competing with Privy-inside-Stripe, not with 2021 seed rounds.
- **First wallet via a Loofta claim can still be a *distribution tactic*** if the job after claim is “get paid / cash out,” because that is a reason to return. It is a failed *thesis* if the job after claim is “now you’re a crypto user.” Arbitrum paid thousands of dollars per retained farmer; a $5 giveaway claim without a payout relationship will look like that, cheaper and equally empty.
- **MoonPay/Privy still sell “create and fund a wallet in one session”** because dapps need funded wallets. That is B2B onboarding infra, CAC borne by the dapp, not a consumer brand.
- **Coinbase** remains the US default funded-wallet on-ramp; it does not need Loofta to onboard Americans. EM first-wallet is already Binance/Bitso/Lemon/GCash.

## If Loofta is “just an off-ramp,” who already owns that?

### Takeaway
Consumer off-ramp is a **licensed, consolidating, high-fixed-cost market** owned by MoonPay, Transak, Ramp Network, Banxa/OSL, Mercuryo, Alchemy Pay, plus **local champions** (Bitso, Lemon, GCash, Coins.ph, PAJ Cash, Airtm P2P). B2B off-ramp is **Bridge/Stripe and BVNK**. US 1:1 is **Coinbase**. Building a greenfield global off-ramp from scratch is a licensing business, not a product feature. Loofta should **rent** last-mile rails and own the claim/payout UX.

### Cited Findings
- White-label 2026 comparison: **Transak** 63+ countries, off-ramp yes; **MoonPay** 100+ business / 180+ consumer, off-ramp yes, consumer app; **Ramp Network** 150+, off-ramp in select regions; **Banxa** 200+ claimed; **Mercuryo** EUR/USD off-ramp only; **BVNK** B2B 130+; **Bridge** (Stripe) B2B global, named virtual accounts; **Iron** (MoonPay) B2B 100+. — [Transak, 8 best ramps 2026](https://transak.com/blog/best-white-label-crypto-on-off-ramp-providers)
- MoonPay: **$643M+**, 30M+ users, **46+ US MTLs + NY BitLicense**, 50-state coverage as of Jun 2025. Card ramps **1.5–4.5% + spread**; documented BTC quote **4.3% over market** before platform fee; total card cost can hit **7–8%**. Bank methods ~1%. Partner apps take a rev-share. — [Spark, 8 May 2026](https://www.spark.money/research/crypto-on-off-ramp-market-landscape)
- Bridge pricing (practitioner): **0.1% conversion at highest volume tier**; USDC/USDT/PYUSD; same-day ACH, FedWire, SEPA, SWIFT; KYB. Not consumer. — [Eco offramps 2026](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210579-best-stablecoin-offramps-2026-cash-out-routes-compared)
- Spark market size estimate: on/off-ramp market **~$8B in 2024**, projected **>$35B by 2033** (this is ramp *revenue/volume category*, not Loofta TAM; treat as order-of-magnitude for the layer, not a company forecast). **86% of crypto companies in Europe faced repeated bank account closures.** US: state-by-state MTL; NY BitLicense. EU: MiCA CASP from 30 Dec 2024. — [Spark](https://www.spark.money/research/crypto-on-off-ramp-market-landscape)
- Wyre’s death is the cautionary off-ramp tale: high correlation to crypto cycles, licensing costs stay fixed when volume dies. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-payments-platform-wyre-shutters-citing-bear-market-conditions)
- Local last-mile (from Meta’s own wallet list and corridor practice): **GCash GCrypto and Coins.ph (PH)**, **Bitso (MX/CO/BR)**, **Lemon/Ripio/Belo (AR)**, **Airtm cashier network (LatAm)**, Loofta’s existing **PAJ Cash (NG)**. ### Inferences
- **Loofta-as-off-ramp loses to MoonPay on licenses and to Bitso/PAJ/GCash on local rails.** The only off-ramp Loofta should “build” is orchestration + UX (which cash-out, which partner, claim-link state machine).
- **B2B senders who just need USDC→USD ACH already have Bridge/Stripe at 0.1% high-volume.** Loofta cannot win SpaceX payroll on price. Loofta can win **pay this email/username who is not a Connect account**.
- **P2P off-ramps (Airtm cashiers, Binance P2P, Nigeria P2P #1) still matter where licensed ramps are thin.** That is messy, fraud-heavy, and exactly where Circle’s Airtm case lives.

## Counter-case: USDC payouts are a feature of rails (Stripe/Circle/PayPal), not a company

### Takeaway
The counter-case is **mostly true in the US/EU and for any platform already on Stripe Connect or PayPal.** It is **false in the last mile** Stripe Connect does not cover (NG, IN, BR absent; individuals-only; US-platform-only; private preview; requires Express Dashboard and an existing wallet) and for **recipients who are an email/@username, not a connected account**. A company remains if it owns **claim + corridor cash-out + sender API** for people who will never be a Stripe connected account. A company does *not* remain if it is “USDC payouts” in the abstract.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe’s own copy: platform stays in fiat; Stripe converts; recipient optionally takes USDC. Access = **contact Stripe sales**, private preview. — [Stripe Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts)
- Circle CPN Managed Payments: “best for banks, PSPs, fintechs, and enterprises who want to enable stablecoin powered payments, **but without directly managing and maintaining digital assets**.” Fiat in, fiat out, USDC hidden. — [CPN Managed Payments](https://www.circle.com/cpn/managed-payments)
- YouTube PYUSD via **PayPal enterprise payouts** — platform adds a checkbox, PayPal is the company. — [PTYcoin / Fortune recap](https://ptycoin.com/en/posts/2026-06-16-stablecoin-payroll-platforms-latam/)
- Meta did not issue a token, run custody, or build Diem-class infra; **RFP to vendors, Stripe + Circle USDC on public chains**. — [CoinDesk, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/tech-giant-meta-starts-paying-some-creators-in-stablecoin-with-stripe-s-support)
- Stripe also bought the two layers a payout app would otherwise own: **Bridge (stablecoin orchestration, $1.1B)** and **Privy (embedded wallets, Jun 2025)**. The stacked Stripe product in 2026 is: Connect payouts + Bridge conversion + Privy wallet + Link. That is an end-to-end competitor to “payout + first wallet + off-ramp.” — [Privy](https://blog.privy.io/blog/announcing-our-acquisition-by-stripe); [Transak comparison](https://transak.com/blog/best-white-label-crypto-on-off-ramp-providers)
- Secondary CoinLaw: Stripe + Shopify **$94B stablecoin payments over two years**, monthly **>$6.3B by Mar 2026**, Shopify USDC-on-Base in **34 countries**, merchant receives **local currency in bank**. That volume is *merchant acceptance*, not creator payouts — do not use as payout TAM. It does show Stripe treating USDC as a checkout feature. — [CoinLaw 2026](https://coinlaw.io/crypto-payments-industry-statistics/)

### Inferences
- **For a US marketplace with KYC’d sellers, Stripe will eat “pay them in USDC.”** Loofta should not sell that buyer a USDC-payout story.
- **For a US sender paying an unregistered email, X handle, or PH/NG creator who is not on Connect,** Stripe’s preview does not replace Loofta. The hole is **non-account recipients + missing Connect countries + consumer claim UX**.
- **PayPal will eat US/EU “get dollars.”** PYUSD 4% yield is PayPal paying users to stay inside PayPal, which makes a Loofta US off-ramp to Venmo a race to the bottom on a rail PayPal already owns.
- **The company-shaped remainder:** Tremendous-like mass payout to individuals in PayPal-failure countries, with a claim link, optional hold-as-dollars, and rented local off-ramp. That is Airtm’s job (workforce) + Tremendous’s job (incentives) + Meta’s missing cash-out. It is not Circle’s job (wholesale) and not MoonPay’s job (ramp widget).

### Gaps
- No public number of platforms live on Stripe Connect stablecoin payouts, or volume.
- No public CPN Fiat Payouts corridor list (which BFIs actually deliver NGN, PHP, ARS).
- Shopify/Stripe $6.3B/month is not payouts; splitting pay-in vs payout was not possible from the secondary source.

## Bottom line: durable USDC-payout niche if Loofta builds the off-ramp? Is crypto onboarding dead?

### Takeaway
**There is a durable niche, and it is not “USDC payouts” or “onboard people to crypto.”** It is **paying individuals in corridors where PayPal/Wise/Stripe bank payouts fail or cost 4–6%+, letting them hold a digital dollar if they want (NG/AR/VE/PH/CO) and cashing them out locally if they don’t.** Building the off-ramp is necessary to complete that job; it will make USDC *invisible* for US/EU recipients and *kept* for EM dollar-savers. **Crypto onboarding as a standalone 2026 thesis is dead** (Privy and Dynamic got acquired; airdrop CAC is thousands of dollars per retained farmer). Onboarding as a *side effect of claiming a payment* is still how Airtm, Rise, and Meta put USDC in new hands — but those users came for dollars, not for crypto.

### Cited Findings
- Recipients who choose to hold: Rise **53%+** stablecoin withdrawals; Airtm workers store USDC until spend; LatAm wallet balances **17.6%** stablecoins; Argentina USDT-as-savings; Nigeria **#1 P2P and #1 cross-border**. — [Circle × Rise](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/rise); [Circle × Airtm](https://www.circle.com/case-studies/airtm); [Chainalysis 2026 LATAM](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/latin-america-crypto-adoption-2026/); [Chainalysis 2026 index](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)
- Recipients who want fiat: Tremendous Venmo/PayPal default; PayPal PYUSD 1:1 inside PayPal; Stripe platform-stays-fiat; Meta still has to tell PH creators to use GCash; CSAO domestic rails make stablecoins a **cross-border** tool (3.2:1). — [Tremendous pricing](https://www.tremendous.com/pricing/); [PayPal PYUSD](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/manage-money/crypto/pyusd); [Stripe Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts); [Chainalysis 2026 CSAO](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/central-southeast-asia-oceania-crypto-adoption-2026/)
- Rails closing the US/EU market: Stripe+Bridge+Privy stack; YouTube via PayPal; Circle CPN Managed Payments. Hole remaining: Connect **excludes NG/IN/BR**, individuals only, US platforms, existing wallet, Express Dashboard. PayPal Nigeria receive only via **Paga, Jan 2026**. — [Stripe Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts); [Privy × Stripe](https://blog.privy.io/blog/announcing-our-acquisition-by-stripe); [Nairametrics, 27 Jan 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/01/27/paypal-return-sparks-boycott-calls-and-mixed-reactions-in-nigeria/)
- Onboarding thesis: Privy → Stripe (Jun 2025), Dynamic → Fireblocks (Oct 2025, secondary), Wyre dead (2023), Arbitrum **~$2,175 / $5,437** CAC, Phantom is a $3B wallet not an onboarding startup. — [Privy](https://blog.privy.io/blog/announcing-our-acquisition-by-stripe); [Kerman Kohli](https://kermankohli.substack.com/p/arbitrums-2175user-acquisition-campaign); [MoonPay on Phantom](https://www.moonpay.com/learn/cryptocurrency/what-is-phantom-wallet)

### Inferences
- **If Loofta builds the off-ramp:** do it as *orchestration of PAJ Cash / Bitso / MoonPay / P2P*, not as a new MTL empire. The product to own is **send-to-@username/email → claim → hold USDC *or* cash out**. That matches Airtm (workforce) and Tremendous (incentives) with a crypto-native claim that those two do not have.
- **Do not pitch US/EU “get paid in USDC.”** Pitch US senders “pay anyone in PH/NG/CO/AR cheaper than PayPal 4–6%.” Pitch EM recipients “keep dollars or cash out today.” Those are two screens, one company.
- **Do not pitch investors “we onboard users to crypto.”** Pitch “we are the payout file for corridors Connect/PayPal skip, and some of those people keep USDC.” First-wallet is a conversion metric, not the business.
- **Kill criteria:** if Stripe Connect adds Nigeria, India, and Brazil, and adds email-only recipients without Express Dashboard, the company-shaped hole shrinks to UX and fraud. Watch that list quarterly.
- **False TAM:** $220B Chainalysis attributed cross-border stablecoins is still mostly USDT long-tail and B2B; Airtm $900M and Rise $0.8–1B are the right *payout* comps. Do not use USDC supply or $70T settlement.
