# Pay-before-account / claim-link competitors (crypto + fiat), as of 2026-10-13

Scope: products that let a sender pay a person who does not yet have an account or wallet, using email, phone, social handle, or a shareable link, with the recipient claiming later. Distinguishes consumer P2P from business bulk API. Nigerian/Africa off-ramps that require a known bank or mobile-money number at send time are a different layer and are called out as such.

Checked in this pass (primary docs/help or named press unless noted): TipLink, Peanut, Coinbase Wallet claim links, Coinbase Business payouts, PayPal consumer + Payouts API, Venmo, Zelle, Wise email/link, Revolut payment links, Telegram/Walt, Sling/Morse, Drop Wallet, Snack Money, Tremendous, Tango, Trolley, Request Finance, Phantom, Farcaster/Warpcast Pay, Base app send, KAST Tag, Strike Lightning, Daimo, X Money / Cross River, Chipper Cash, Flutterwave Send App, tip.cc, Bitrefill (via tip.cc), Minisend, Wyre/SendWyre, Helio/Tango/PayPal Enterprise as adjacent. Searched and not found as pending-person products: Fuse (consumer send-to-unregistered), Glider (as named; Glide is existing-user send). Colosseum Copilot PAT was not set in this environment, so Colosseum project lists were not enumerated.

Verdict preview: **"Pay someone who does not have an account yet" is crowded and decades-old in fiat, and is now standard in several crypto wallets too.** The claim-link layer itself is not differentiated. Nigerian off-ramps are plentiful but they are a different layer (known bank / BVN / mobile-money at payout time). The remaining open slice is the **intersection**: a business bulk API to unverified social handles or emails, OAuth-bound claim, then recipient choice of USDC *and* US P2P apps *and* African/Nigerian cash-out on one screen. Each piece exists separately. No sourced product in this pass assembles all of them.

## For each product: identifier, KYC/app before claim, claim destinations, fees, unclaimed-fund behavior, scale, country coverage

### Takeaway
Almost every major P2P rail already lets you pay a person who is not enrolled. The dominant model is escrow + join-this-app-to-claim + auto-return in 7–30 days. The rare exceptions that let a non-user cash out without joining the sender's app are Peanut (claim to SEPA/ACH if the sender is verified) and, historically, Sling Links (2024 copy said claim to bank without the app; 2026 Morse copy says download and join — conflict). Name systems (ENS, .sol, Lightning addresses, Phantom usernames, KAST Tags, Farcaster FIDs) resolve to an *existing* wallet or account and are not pending-person products.

### Cited Findings

**Fiat consumer P2P**

- **PayPal (consumer)**: send to an email or phone that is not on a verified PayPal account; status is pending/unclaimed; recipient must set up or verify a PayPal account. Sender can cancel. PayPal automatically cancels pending/unclaimed payments after 30 days and returns money to the original payment method (bank refunds up to 5 business days; card refunds up to 30 days). — [PayPal Help](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/why-is-the-payment-i-sent-pending-or-unclaimed-can-i-cancel-it-help111)
- **Venmo**: send to an unverified or unregistered phone/email; payment stays Pending until the recipient finishes Venmo setup. Sender can "Take Back." If unclaimed or not taken back within 30 days, it expires and returns to the funding source. Take-back is only available if that phone/email is not associated with an active Venmo account. — [Venmo Help](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/my-personal-venmo-payment-is-pending-vhel276)
- **Cash App**: send to email, phone, or $cashtag. Secondary consumer write-ups treat completed Cash App payments as irreversible. Official US unclaimed-expiry for *unregistered* recipients was not confirmed from a primary Cash App help page in this pass. — [Experian on P2P reversals](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/what-if-i-send-app-payment-to-wrong-person/)
- **Zelle**: identifier is a US mobile number or email. An unenrolled recipient gets email/SMS and must enroll via their bank using that exact identifier. If they do not enroll within 14 days, the payment expires and is refunded. Sender can cancel only while the recipient remains unenrolled. Once enrolled, funds go to the linked bank and cannot be cancelled. US-only. — [Zelle FAQ](https://www.zelle.com/faq/what-if-person-i-am-sending-money-hasnt-enrolled-zelle)
- **Wise "Pay by email"**: website only. Recipient is emailed and must create a Wise account with the **same** email, then claim to Wise or an external bank. Default Wise Help Centre: recipient has **10 days** after Wise receives the sender's deposit; otherwise Wise cancels and refunds. Wise UK help on the same article ID says **7 calendar days**. Conflict: treat as 7–10 days depending on locale/page. Not available for sending from BRL/INR or to LKR, BDT, COP, CNY, UAH, BWP, GHS, or USD over Global Swift. NGN is not mentioned as blocked or supported on the fetched page. — [Wise Help (10 days)](https://wise.com/help/articles/2932105/how-can-i-send-money-to-an-email-address); [Wise UK Help (7 days)](https://wise.com/uk/help/articles/2932105/how-can-i-send-money-to-an-email-address)
- **Wise "Send via link"** (personal accounts): recipient opens a link, enters email/password and bank details, claims. Limits: max 200 USD equivalent, 5 active links, **5 calendar days** validity. Cancellable until claimed. Still rolling out. — [Wise send-with-link](https://wise.com/help/articles/2lMLg5SpSbLNtWWA73O2Ot/how-do-i-send-money-with-a-link)
- **Wise Business batch "send by email"**: CSV of emails; recipients get a secure link to enter bank details; **1 week** to enter details before cancel/refund. Not available to BDT, BWP, COP, GHS, LKR, PEN, UAH, or USD outside the US. — [Wise batch templates](https://wise.com/help/articles/2942721/what-are-the-different-batch-template-options)
- **Revolut payment links**: "You don't need any bank details when you transfer money via a payment link, just your recipient's phone number or email… The person you're sending to can then claim that money, even if they're not a Revolut customer." Claim destinations on Revolut's own US money-transfer page are not enumerated beyond claiming the money. A Wise comparison article (secondary) says Revolut API payout links expire within 7 days unless changed in the API. — [Revolut US money transfer](https://www.revolut.com/en-US/money-transfer/); [Wise on Revolut links](https://wise.com/gb/blog/revolut-payment-links)
- **X Money**: Cross River Bank announced it powers X Money with FDIC-insured deposits, Visa debit, and in-app P2P as "the first P2P payments experience built into a U.S. social media platform." Identifier is an X @handle among enrolled users. A third-party UsePaid/SendMoney guide says a payee without X Money cannot claim and that unclaimed X Money payments expire after about 14 days; that is **not** an X primary source. Secondary consumer blogs say the recipient must already have an X Money account. I found **no X or Cross River primary source** that X Money can pay an unenrolled handle (pending/claim model). Coverage: US embedded banking via Cross River. — [Cross River press](https://www.crossriver.com/newsroom/cross-river-powers-x-money-first-p2p-payments-experience-built-into-us-social-media-platform); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/x-money-adds-cross-river-as-sponsor-bank); [SendMoney/UsePaid docs](https://sendmoney.fun/docs)

**Crypto claim links and social-identity wallets**

- **TipLink (Solana)**: the link *is* a wallet (keypair in the URL). Identifier is a bearer URL, optionally emailed. Recipient needs no prior wallet; they open the link. Non-escrow: anyone with the URL can withdraw. Email TipLinks go through a secure enclave so TipLink Corp "never sees the private keys." Docs recommend Escrow for recoverability, delivery failure, and multisig safety. Escrow can be claimed back by the depositor. API (`@tiplink/api`) creates TipLinks, campaigns, dispensers (captcha/fingerprint), and `mail` / `mailEscrow`. Email API needs a backend key requested from TipLink. Solana-only. I found no 2025–2026 volume figure in this pass. — [Email TipLinks](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/products/email-tiplinks); [Client API](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/products/client); [Email escrow example](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/extended-examples/email-escrow-example)
- **Peanut (peanut.me)**: bearer link or QR. Creating a link and claiming into Peanut is free; "Links do not expire." Sender can reclaim until claimed. Recipient without a Peanut account can select "Claim to bank account": SEPA → EUR (under 20 minutes for 90% of transfers); ACH → USD (1–3 business days). "No app, no account, and no verification required for the recipient (as long as you, the creator, are verified)." One side must be verified. Pix/Mercado Pago: claim into Peanut first, then pay out. Legacy peanut.to docs still list Europe/SEPA, US, Mexico as current bank-claim countries and "other LATAM and African countries: coming soon," while also listing Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Peru under LATAM — internal conflict on the older docs page. Current peanut.me geography: bank routes for eligible verified accounts are SEPA, ACH/wire, Faster Payments (GBP), SPEI (MXN), COP; UK residents and New York residents are excluded from bank routes. — [Peanut Help: send-money-link](https://peanut.me/en/help/send-money-link); [Peanut geographies](https://peanut.me/en/help/supported-geographies); [Peanut receive docs](https://docs.peanut.to/how-to-receive-money-via-peanut)
- **Coinbase Wallet / claim links**: share a link; recipient downloads Coinbase Wallet (or imports an existing seed) and taps Claim. Coinbase pays network fees; feature is free. Supported assets listed on the live claim page: native USDC on Base, ETH on Base, native/bridged USDC on Polygon, MATIC. Sender can reclaim by opening the link and claiming back. **Unclaimed for two weeks → auto-return** on the claim page. A separate Base-app help article says unclaimed funds return after **5 days**. Conflict: 5 days vs 2 weeks depending on product surface (Wallet claim page vs Base app help). Claim page still live as of this fetch. — [Coinbase Wallet claim](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet/claim); [Base app send/receive help](https://help.coinbase.com/en/wallet/sending-and-receiving/how-do-i-send-and-receive-crypto-through-wallet)
- **Coinbase Business global payouts** (announced 16 Oct 2025): send USDC to an onchain address **or email**. "If your recipient doesn’t have a crypto wallet, they can receive the payment via email, create a Coinbase account for free to claim their funds, and easily cash out in their local market." Payouts API for on-demand, batch, or scheduled payouts. Coinbase Business was in alpha for US early customers at announcement. Fees: "Log into your Coinbase Business account to view current fees" — not published on the marketing page. — [Coinbase Business blog](https://www.coinbase.com/en-gb/blog/introducing-a-powerful-suite-of-business-payment-tools-on-coinbase-business); [Finextra reprint](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/107570/coinbase-rolls-out-business-payment-tools); [Coinbase Business payments](https://www.coinbase.com/en-br/business/payments)
- **Telegram Wallet / Walt** (rebranded Wallet → Walt on 28 Sep 2026): identifier is a Telegram contact/username, not public email. (1) Payment-link / "Share with": 7 days to claim; if no Walt account, create via the link; **group-chat links are first-come bearer** ("any member of the chat can claim it, but the funds will only go to the first person to tap Claim"); no fees, limits apply. (2) Direct contact send to custodial Walt: if recipient never used Walt, 14 days to create account **and complete identity verification**, else auto-return; sender cannot cancel. (3) DeFi Account: escrow smart contract; 14 days to create DeFi Account and accept; cannot cancel once sent; reject or timeout returns funds. — [Walt withdrawals](https://help.walt.io/article/13-withdrawals); [DeFi send to Telegram users](https://help.walt.io/article/233-send-tokens-to-telegram-users); [Walt rebrand](https://help.walt.io/article/1078-walt-everything-you-need-to-know)
- **Sling Money / Morse**: Sling rebranded to Morse (founder post 15 Apr 2026). 2024 Sling Link withdraw post: recipient provides bank details + email; "Sling currently operates in 75+ countries"; no app mentioned. 2026 Morse "send a Sling Link" post (page stamped Feb 2026): "Your recipient will be able to easily download and join Sling to claim their funds." **Conflict on whether app signup is required.** Morse help (Apr 2026): 140+ countries for P2P; add/withdraw to local methods in 70+ of those / 40+ currencies; identity verification (name, phone, DOB, address, selfie, photo ID) required; 18+. Withdraw methods: bank, debit card, mobile money, Pix, or stablecoin, depending on country. I did not find a Nigeria-specific Morse claim article in this pass. — [Withdraw from a Sling Link (2024)](https://morsemoney.com/blog/how-to-withdraw-from-a-morse-link); [Send a Sling Link (updated 2026)](https://morsemoney.com/blog/how-to-send-a-morse-link); [Who can use Morse](https://support.morsemoney.com/en/articles/13752343-who-can-use-morse); [Withdraw Morse balance](https://support.morsemoney.com/en/articles/13761104-withdraw-money-from-your-morse-balance); [Sling is now Morse](https://morsemoney.com/blog/sling-is-now-morse)
- **Drop Wallet**: send to X / Telegram / Discord handle, phone, or email **even if the recipient has never used Drop**. Funds sit in a non-custodial on-chain Deposit Vault (Solana and Base escrow; verified tokens only; other chains need an existing Drop account or a wallet address). Recipient signs up and proves ownership (OAuth for socials, SMS for phone, email link). Only the verified owner can claim. Identifiers hashed on-chain. Escrow deposits/claims/reclaims are fee-sponsored. Sender can cancel before claim; after 325 days sender can reclaim; after 365 days Drop may recover unclaimed escrow. Consumer wallet; docs list "Giveaway / Distribute to many recipients" as a vault use case. I did not fetch a public bulk-business API. — [Drop Send](https://docs.dropwallet.app/wallet/send); [Deposit Vault](https://docs.dropwallet.app/features/deposit-vault)
- **Snack Money**: production API to pay X, Farcaster, email, web/domain, plus A2A skills for GitHub and Instagram. USDC on Base and Solana. Batch endpoints: `/payments/x/batch-pay`, `/payments/farcaster/batch-pay`, `/payments/email/batch-pay`. Getting-started example amount is 0.01 USDC. CLI `batch-send` for airdrops/bulk. Designed for "microtransaction and reward distribution." First-party docs fetched in this pass do **not** describe claim UX, OAuth, unclaimed expiry, or published fees. — [Snack Money API](https://docs.snack.money/api-reference/introduction); [Getting started](https://docs.snack.money/introduction/getting-started); [batch-send CLI](https://docs.snack.money/cli/batch-send); [A2A](https://docs.snack.money/examples/a2a)
- **Phantom**: send to a wallet address or **existing Phantom username**. Help lists "a Phantom user" as a destination, not an unregistered X handle. US users can send to a linked bank. This is existing-user resolution, not pay-a-pending-person. — [Phantom send help](https://help.phantom.com/articles/5530158379539)
- **Farcaster / Warpcast Pay**: in-app USDC on Base to a profile's most recently connected address. Requires the recipient already have a Farcaster identity and a connected wallet. Co-founder Dan Romero: "No need to look up an address." This is username resolution, not pay-a-pending-person. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/302665/farcaster-is-getting-in-app-usdc-payments)
- **Base app**: Coinbase help for sending/receiving through the Base/Wallet app includes send-via-link (up to $500 in some assets) and the 5-day unclaimed-return note cited above. Basename/ENS/cb.id resolution is address lookup for people who already registered a name, not pending-person. — [Base app send/receive](https://help.coinbase.com/en/wallet/sending-and-receiving/how-do-i-send-and-receive-crypto-through-wallet)
- **KAST Tag**: send by KAST Tag, name, email, phone, or QR **to a KAST user**. First-come handle. Blog: "Anyone with a phone number and KAST account can receive funds." Existing-user, not escrow for non-users. — [KAST Tag blog](https://www.kast.xyz/blog/kast-tag-send-money-instantly-and-for-free-to-any-kast-user)
- **Strike / Lightning address**: every Strike user gets `username@strike.me`. Sender pays any Lightning address; recipient must already have a Lightning wallet / Strike account. **Not** a pending-claim model. — [Strike Lightning](https://strike.me/learn/what-is-a-lightning-address/)
- **Daimo**: payment API uses explicit destination addresses or session deposit addresses; fiat on-ramp is hosted KYC + local rails into a destination. I found no documented send-to-unregistered-email claim link. 2025 "universal addresses" work is a destination-resolution protocol, not a pending-person product in the fetched material. — [Daimo API overview](https://docs.daimo.com/api-reference/overview); [Daimo sessions](https://docs.daimo.com/guides/sessions)
- **tip.cc (Discord)**: custodial wallet bound to Discord ID. Send to anyone on Discord "without having a wallet"; they later `$withdraw` to an external address. 500+ coins; "no minimums and no fees on tips"; network fees on withdraw. Discord-only; not a public business payout API. Cash-out guide points users to Bitrefill gift cards or exchanges, not a built-in bank/P2P picker. — [tip.cc](https://tip.cc/); [Terms](https://tip.cc/terms); [Cash out guide](https://tip.cc/guides/cashing-out-coins)
- **Helio / MoonPay Commerce**: not fetched as a payout product in this pass; prior-category check and product positioning is merchant-collect (buyer pays merchant), opposite direction from Loofta. Treat as not a pending-person *payout*.
- **Bitrefill**: tip.cc's cash-out guide describes Bitrefill as buying gift cards / mobile top-ups with crypto. Recipient gets a merchant gift card, not USDC or a bank payout, and does not "claim" a pending identity. — [tip.cc cash-out](https://tip.cc/guides/cashing-out-coins)
- **ENS / Bonfida SNS / .sol / Basenames / Lens / Zora**: human-readable names that resolve to an already-registered wallet or social profile. Paying `alice.eth` or `alice.sol` requires Alice to have minted/bound the name. Not pay-before-account. (Checked as identifier systems, not as claim products.)
- **Solana Blinks / Dialect**: in-feed transaction buttons for people who already have a wallet. Phantom's older Blink support is widely reported as discontinued; I did not re-fetch Phantom's Blink docs in this pass (gap).
- **Minisend** (adjacent last-mile, not pay-before-account): Farcaster/Base mini-app that converts existing Base USDC to KES via M-Pesa or NGN via Nigerian bank. Recipient already has USDC. Relevant as the African cash-out rail sitting *after* a claim, not as a claim-link. — [Minisend](https://www.minisend.fun/)

**Africa send products that look similar but are not claim-links**

- **Chipper Cash send to non-Chipper**: sender enters the recipient's **bank or mobile-money number**. Recipient does not need Chipper. This is known-destination off-ramp, not "pay an email and they claim later." Funds can take up to 4 business days. — [Chipper non-user send](https://support.chippercash.com/en/articles/6282268-how-to-send-money-directly-to-non-chipper-recipients-bank-or-mobile-money-account-in-other-african-countries)

- **Flutterwave Send App**: transfers to bank, mobile money, or cash pickup from supported send countries (incl. Nigeria) after the sender signs up and verifies identity. Recipient destination is known at send time. — [Send App](https://send.flutterwave.com/)

**Recipient-choice incentive platforms (fiat, email)**

- **Tremendous**: business emails a reward; recipient chooses among products the sender enabled. Catalog includes gift cards, prepaid Visa, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, US ACH, international bank (help table: GBP, EUR, AUD, CAD, INR, MXN, BRL), and Asian wallets (GCash, GoPay, Alipay, DANA, LinkAja, Naver Pay, OVO, ShopeePay, TrueMoney, etc.). Monetary options typically 4% (mins $0.25–$2.50) billed to sender on the help table fetched via search; Asian wallets 5%. **No USDC/crypto row and no NGN/KES/GHS bank row** on that monetary table. 230+ countries/regions for *some* catalog item is marketing language on the product page, not a claim that every country has bank payout. A Sep 2026 FlipEx advertorial describes Nigerians selling Tremendous Visa cards for Naira — evidence that NGN cash-out is a third-party workaround, not a Tremendous rail. — [Tremendous monetary options](https://www.tremendous.com/products/monetary-options/); [Tremendous monetary help](https://help.tremendous.com/hc/en-us/articles/41472317536787-Monetary-reward-options); [Tremendous payout methods docs](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/specifying-payout-options); [FlipEx/Tremendous Visa-to-NGN (secondary)](https://business.sherbrookerecord.com/observerreporter/article/globeprwire-2026-9-23-solving-the-tremendous-visa-card-dilemma-where-to-use-your-tremendous-visa-card)
- **Tango Card**: Reward Link (recipient chooses gift cards/prepaid/donations via email or SMS); Global Choice Link (200+ countries / 50+ currencies per homepage); Payouts Link (ACH, debit deposit, PayPal, donations). Bulk via portal or API. Email/SMS identifier. No sourced USDC option. — [Tango](https://www.tangocard.com/)

### Inferences
- Unclaimed windows cluster at ~5–7 days (Wise link, Revolut API per Wise, Telegram payment links, possibly Base-app links), 10 days (Wise email on the default help page), 14 days (Zelle, Telegram direct/DeFi, Coinbase Wallet claim page), and 30 days (PayPal, Venmo). Peanut never expires. Drop is an outlier at ~1 year.
- "Claim into whatever rail you already use" is rare. Incumbents force join-our-network. Peanut is the cleanest consumer exception for bank cash-out without an app. Tremendous is the business exception for gift cards + US P2P + selected banks.
- Identifier systems (ENS, SNS, Lightning, Phantom username, KAST, Farcaster FID) should not be counted as Loofta competitors for the pending-person thesis. They compete only for "pay an @name" among people who already opted in.
- Africa "send to someone not on the app" usually means "I already have their bank or momo number" (Chipper, Flutterwave Send). That is the off-ramp layer the founder is counting as "thousands of companies," and it does **not** implement claim-links.

## Which products let a business bulk-pay ~1,000 identifiers via API?

### Takeaway
This slot is **not empty**. Fiat already has mature bulk pay-by-email APIs (PayPal Payouts, Tremendous, Tango, Trolley, Wise Business email-batch). Crypto now has Coinbase Business (email → Coinbase), TipLink Pro (links/email/campaigns), Snack Money (X/Farcaster/GitHub/email/domain batch USDC), and Request Finance (recipient list → USDC or bank). Loofta's "pay 1,000 handles" target collides first with Snack Money + Coinbase Business + PayPal/Tremendous, not with Nigerian off-ramp APIs (those need bank details up front).

### Cited Findings

**Business APIs that pay an identifier before the recipient has fully onboarded**

- **PayPal Payouts API** (ex Mass Pay): batch to `EMAIL`, `PHONE`, `USER_HANDLE` (Venmo), or `PAYPAL_ID`. If the recipient does not have a Venmo account, "they receive instructions to create one and claim the payout." Venmo payouts US-only. Unclaimed items stay `UNCLAIMED` and auto-return after 30 days; cancellable via `POST /v1/payments/payouts-item/{id}/cancel`. Large batches also via DropZone CSV. Access is a gated business product. — [PayPal Payouts](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payouts); [Payouts to Venmo](https://developer.paypal.com/payouts/use-payouts/venmo); [Cancel unclaimed](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments.payouts-batch/v1/payouts-item-cancel); [Handle unclaimed](https://developer.paypal.com/payouts/use-payouts/handle-unclaimed-payouts)
- **Tremendous API / CSV**: email delivery; recipient chooses from enabled products (see previous section). Explicitly built for bulk incentives. Multi-product rewards let the recipient pick. — [Tremendous monetary options](https://www.tremendous.com/products/monetary-options/); [Tremendous payout methods](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/specifying-payout-options); [Multi-product rewards](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/creating-multi-product-rewards-wip)
- **Tango**: Reward Link / Global Choice Link / Payouts Link; catalog + API. Email or SMS. — [Tango](https://www.tangocard.com/)
- **Trolley**: create recipients by email; `POST /v1/recipients/solicit` emails an invite so they self-onboard bank/tax/payout details. Invitations are **not** auto-sent on import. Recipients must already exist as Trolley records. Venmo payouts available for US merchants (PayPal Payouts under the hood). — [Trolley solicit](https://support.trolley.com/s/article/Request-Bank-Information-from-Incomplete-Recipients); [Trolley Venmo](https://support.trolley.com/s/article/Setting-up-Venmo)
- **Wise Business batch email template**: emails + amounts; each recipient gets a link to enter bank details; 1 week then refund. Not a social-handle API. — [Wise batch templates](https://wise.com/help/articles/2942721/what-are-the-different-batch-template-options)
- **Request Finance Direct Payments**: upload a recipient list; each recipient chooses USDC/USDT to a wallet **or** local-currency bank (190+ countries, 25+ currencies) in one batch. Stablecoin payouts free; bank = percentage + rail fee (blog: flat 0.5% fiat off-ramp on Growth+; subscriptions from $50/month). **Not** pay-a-handle: the business already has a list of people, and recipients supply wallet or bank details when they register. — [Request Finance mass payouts](https://www.requestfinance.com/use-cases/mass-payouts); [Request Finance mass-payments guide](https://www.requestfinance.com/blog/crypto-mass-payments-guide)
- **Coinbase Business Payouts / Transfers**: email or onchain USDC; batch/scheduled API; sales-gated / alpha US at Oct 2025 launch. Recipients without a wallet create a Coinbase account and cash out locally (blog). — [Coinbase Business blog](https://www.coinbase.com/en-gb/blog/introducing-a-powerful-suite-of-business-payment-tools-on-coinbase-business)
- **TipLink Pro / `@tiplink/api`**: campaigns, `addEntries`, dispensers. Email API (`mail` / `mailEscrow`) needs a backend key. Positioned as send-to-non-crypto-users at campaign scale (airdrops/NFT drops more than contractor payroll). — [Client API](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/products/client); [Email TipLinks](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/products/email-tiplinks)
- **Snack Money**: documented batch endpoints for X, Farcaster, email (plus A2A GitHub/Instagram/web). Closest **bulk pay-a-social-handle** API found. Traction beyond docs/hackathon-adjacent positioning is not sourced. Fees and a first-class 1,000-handle limit are not published on the pages fetched. — [Snack Money API](https://docs.snack.money/api-reference/introduction); [batch-send CLI](https://docs.snack.money/cli/batch-send)

**Not this layer (known destination required)**

- Chipper, Flutterwave Send, and typical Nigerian/Africa off-ramp APIs pay a **bank account or mobile-money number you already have**. They do not implement claim-links. — [Chipper non-user send](https://support.chippercash.com/en/articles/6282268-how-to-send-money-directly-to-non-chipper-recipients-bank-or-mobile-money-account-in-other-african-countries); [Send App](https://send.flutterwave.com/)
- Helio / Coinbase payment links / Chipper Payment Link: collect-side (someone pays you).
- Drop Wallet giveaways: consumer feature; no public bulk-business API fetched.

### Inferences
- A founder comparing Loofta only to "thousands of Nigerian off-ramps" is looking at the wrong layer. Off-ramps assume destination KYC data at send time. The claim-link / pay-a-pending-person layer is a **global** category already occupied by PayPal, Tremendous, Wise Business, Coinbase, TipLink, and Snack Money.
- Scale readiness differs: PayPal/Tremendous/Trolley/Wise are enterprise-hardened. Snack Money is documented for micro-rewards (0.01 USDC examples). TipLink Pro is built for campaigns. Coinbase Business was still alpha/US in Oct 2025.
- Request Finance is the closest **crypto+bank recipient choice** at business scale, but it is list-and-onboard, not pay-@alice-on-X.

### Gaps
- Pricing for Coinbase Business email payouts, TipLink Pro, and Snack Money is not published on the pages fetched.
- No sourced statement that Snack Money, Drop, or TipLink support 1,000-handle jobs as a first-class limit.
- Grey, Eversend, Paystack Transfers, Fincra, Bitnob were not searched in this pass for an email-claim primitive (Chipper and Flutterwave Send were; they are known-destination).
- Revolut Business payout-link API was cited only via a Wise secondary article, not Revolut's own API docs.

## Which bind the handle to a verified human vs anyone-can-claim a link?

### Takeaway
Three models, all common: (1) **bearer link** — whoever opens the URL wins (TipLink non-escrow, Peanut, Coinbase Wallet links, Telegram group-chat links, Sling/Morse Links); (2) **account-bound email/phone** — claimant must control that inbox/SIM and usually join the provider (PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Wise, Coinbase email, TipLink email/escrow); (3) **OAuth / platform-identity** — claimant proves the social account (Drop Wallet; tip.cc via Discord ID; Telegram username; Snack Money *pays* platform IDs but claim-proof was not first-party documented). OAuth-to-handle is already implemented and is not a moat by itself.

### Cited Findings
- TipLink non-escrow: the URL is the keypair; "Anyone can withdraw with the URL." Email + escrow exist because email delivery and recoverability are unsafe as pure bearer instruments. — [Email TipLinks](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/products/email-tiplinks); [Email escrow example](https://docs.tiplink.io/docs/extended-examples/email-escrow-example)
- Peanut: a link or QR "contains money"; share via message, email, or print; recipient claims — no app required if claiming to bank and sender is verified. — [Peanut Help](https://peanut.me/en/help/send-money-link)
- Coinbase Wallet links: any holder of the link claims in Wallet; 2-week return on the claim page; phishing caution on Base-app help (legitimate hosts `wallet.coinbase.com` or `go.cb-w.com/claim`). — [Claim page](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet/claim); [Base app help](https://help.coinbase.com/en/wallet/sending-and-receiving/how-do-i-send-and-receive-crypto-through-wallet)
- Telegram group payment links: "any member of the chat can claim it, but the funds will only go to the first person to tap Claim." Direct-to-contact is bound to that Telegram user + KYC for custodial Walt. — [Walt withdrawals](https://help.walt.io/article/13-withdrawals)
- Wise: new account "must use the same email address." — [Wise Help](https://wise.com/help/articles/2932105/how-can-i-send-money-to-an-email-address)
- Zelle: must enroll the **exact** email or US mobile used. — [Zelle FAQ](https://www.zelle.com/faq/what-if-person-i-am-sending-money-hasnt-enrolled-zelle)
- Venmo: pending until that phone/email is verified on a Venmo account. — [Venmo Help](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/my-personal-venmo-payment-is-pending-vhel276)
- Drop: "Social handles: Connect their X, Telegram, or Discord account; Phone: SMS; Email: link. Only the verified owner can claim. Identifiers are hashed on-chain." — [Deposit Vault](https://docs.dropwallet.app/features/deposit-vault); [Drop Send](https://docs.dropwallet.app/wallet/send)
- Snack Money: API pays `x`, `farcaster`, `github`, `email`, `web`, `instagram` identities. First-party claim-binding docs were not fetched. — [Snack Money API](https://docs.snack.money/api-reference/introduction)
- tip.cc: wallet is the Discord user; you cannot claim someone else's tip without their Discord account. — [tip.cc](https://tip.cc/)
- Farcaster Pay: bound to FID + connected wallet, but the human must already be on Farcaster. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/302665/farcaster-is-getting-in-app-usdc-payments)
- Tremendous / Tango: email (or SMS) delivery; redemption is bound to the emailed reward link, not to OAuth of a social handle. — [Tremendous payout methods](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/specifying-payout-options); [Tango](https://www.tangocard.com/)

### Inferences
- For **business** payouts, bearer links are a liability (forwarding, phishing, first-click-wins). PayPal/Tremendous/Wise/Zelle all bind to email/phone for that reason. TipLink's own docs push escrow/email for recoverability.
- Binding to X via OAuth is table stakes in 2025–2026 crypto (Drop; Snack Money at least *addresses* X users). Differentiation is not "we OAuth the handle."
- Telegram is the strongest **native** social-identity rail at consumer scale (username is the account), but it is closed: you pay inside Telegram, into Walt/DeFi, with Telegram KYC on the custodial path.

### Gaps
- Snack Money's production claim UX (OAuth vs magic link vs custodial) was not fetched from a first-party claim guide.
- Did not verify whether TipLink Pro or Coinbase Business can bind an X/Telegram handle rather than email.
- X Money handle-binding for unenrolled users: no primary source found.
- Boon / ArcPay / Cukka-style OAuth-claim protocols were not searched in this pass.

## Which offer recipient choice across crypto + fiat P2P apps + local bank in one claim?

### Takeaway
**No sourced product in this pass offers USDC + Venmo/Cash App/Revolut + local bank (especially NGN) on one claim screen.** Closest assemblages: Tremendous (Venmo/Cash App/PayPal + selected banks + gift cards; no crypto, no NGN in the monetary table); Request Finance (USDC/USDT **or** bank in 190+ countries; not P2P apps, not handle-based); Peanut (wallet or SEPA/ACH/MX/GBP/COP bank for eligible users; Africa "coming soon" on older docs); Sling/Morse (bank / mobile money / wallet after joining); Coinbase (USDC into Coinbase, then local cash-out — one custodial funnel).

### Cited Findings
- Tremendous monetary catalog: Instant debit, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, US bank, GBP/EUR/AUD/CAD/INR/MXN/BRL banks, plus listed Asian wallets. No USDC row. No NGN/KES/GHS bank row. — [Tremendous monetary help](https://help.tremendous.com/hc/en-us/articles/41472317536787-Monetary-reward-options)

- Peanut: Peanut balance, bank (SEPA/ACH and, for account holders, Faster Payments/SPEI/COP), external claim routes. No Venmo/Cash App. Africa bank claims still "coming soon" on peanut.to receive docs. — [Peanut Help](https://peanut.me/en/help/send-money-link); [Peanut receive](https://docs.peanut.to/how-to-receive-money-via-peanut)

## Dead and failed attempts, and why

### Takeaway
The claim-link *idea* did not fail. Wyre died as on/off-ramp infrastructure in the 2022–2023 crypto winter after a collapsed $1.5B Bolt deal, explicitly "not due to any regulatory agency direction." Coinbase Wallet links were not killed; the claim page is still live. Sling rebranded to Morse and kept Links. Peanut's older peanut.to UI is leftover docs, but peanut.me is live. Social tipping mostly survived as custodial bots (tip.cc) or shrank to in-app USDC (Farcaster), not as open payout networks.

### Cited Findings
- **Wyre / SendWyre**: Bolt scrapped the ~$1.5B acquisition on 9 Sep 2022 amid crashing crypto/fintech valuations. — [Reuters Bolt/Wyre](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/payments-company-bolt-scraps-15-bln-deal-buy-crypto-firm-wyre-2022-09-10/)
- Wyre announced wind-down 16 Jun 2023 "due to market conditions… This decision is not due to any regulatory agency direction." Withdrawals through 14 Jul 2023, later extended to 31 Jul 2023. Peak ~200 staff, ~50 by Apr 2023. Blockworks: "deep seated financial and reputational problems." Reuters: crypto winter after failed Bolt deal, FTX bankruptcy, SEC suits as backdrop — not a claim that those suits caused the wind-down. — [Wyre Medium](https://medium.com/sendwyre/wyre-operations-update-712a6d084229); [Extension](https://medium.com/sendwyre/wyre-update-wind-down-extension-to-july-31-2023-c2140008955c); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/wyre-winds-down); [Reuters wind-down](https://www.reuters.com/technology/wyre-winding-down-crypto-winter-bites-2023-06-16/)
- **Coinbase Wallet links**: still documented (live claim page + 2-week return). Wallet product is also described under the Base app. — [Claim page](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet/claim)
- **Sling → Morse**: brand change Apr 2026, not a shutdown; Links remain in 2026 posts. — [Sling is now Morse](https://morsemoney.com/blog/sling-is-now-morse); [Send a Sling Link](https://morsemoney.com/blog/how-to-send-a-morse-link)
- **X Money delay**: talked about for years; Cross River live embedded banking is 2026 press. Still no sourced pending-unenrolled or crypto. Delay looks like licensing/sponsor-bank caution, not a killed claim-link. — [Cross River](https://www.crossriver.com/newsroom/cross-river-powers-x-money-first-p2p-payments-experience-built-into-us-social-media-platform); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/x-money-adds-cross-river-as-sponsor-bank)

### Inferences
- Wyre is a bad analogy for "claim links don't work." It was custody + fiat rails + a failed acquisition in a funding winter.
- What *does* stay small or risky: bearer-link phishing (Coinbase itself warns about fake claim hosts), social-tip tokens that never became payroll rails, and consumer apps that cannot get Venmo/Cash App as destinations without PayPal/Tremendous-class partnerships.
- Incumbents keep shipping the feature (PayPal 30-day unclaimed, Coinbase Business email, Telegram 7/14-day, Wise email/link). Demand for pending-person payout is evidenced by persistence, not by a graveyard.

### Gaps

- Glider, Fuse, KAST claim-link failures: products either not found or existing-user-only; no shutdown post-mortem found.
- Wyre's specific "send to email" product behaviour at death was not separately documented in the pages fetched (Wyre was broader infra).
- Phantom Blinks discontinuation not re-fetched from primary Phantom docs in this pass.

## What exactly is unowned? Honest crowding verdict

### Takeaway
**Crowded, if the pitch is "pay an @username or email before they have an account, they claim later."** That sentence already describes PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Wise, Revolut, Coinbase, Telegram/Walt, TipLink, Peanut, Sling/Morse, Tremendous, Tango, and Drop, with Snack Money as the closest bulk-handle API. It is a **feature**, not a category moat. **Still open as a narrow intersection:** a business API that pays 1,000 *unverified social handles*, binds the claim with OAuth, and lets the recipient pick **USDC or US P2P or African/Nigerian cash-out** without the sender collecting bank/BVN. That intersection was not found. It is also assemblable by Tremendous (add USDC + NGN), Coinbase (add handle + multi-rail claim), Peanut/Morse (add bulk handle API), or Snack Money/Drop (add fiat rails).

### Cited Findings
- Consumer pending-person is standard: PayPal 30-day email/phone — [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/why-is-the-payment-i-sent-pending-or-unclaimed-can-i-cancel-it-help111); Venmo 30-day — [Venmo](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/my-personal-venmo-payment-is-pending-vhel276); Zelle 14-day — [Zelle](https://www.zelle.com/faq/what-if-person-i-am-sending-money-hasnt-enrolled-zelle); Wise 10-day email (7-day on UK page) — [Wise](https://wise.com/help/articles/2932105/how-can-i-send-money-to-an-email-address); Revolut claim-without-account — [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/en-US/money-transfer/); Coinbase Wallet 2-week link — [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet/claim); Telegram 7/14-day — [Walt](https://help.walt.io/article/13-withdrawals).
- Business bulk-by-email is standard: PayPal Payouts — [PayPal](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payouts); Tremendous choice — [Tremendous](https://www.tremendous.com/products/monetary-options/); Wise Business email batch — [Wise](https://wise.com/help/articles/2942721/what-are-the-different-batch-template-options); Coinbase Business email USDC — [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/en-gb/blog/introducing-a-powerful-suite-of-business-payment-tools-on-coinbase-business); Snack Money handle batch — [Snack Money](https://docs.snack.money/cli/batch-send).
- Handle-bound crypto escrow exists: Drop OAuth vault — [Drop](https://docs.dropwallet.app/features/deposit-vault).
- Full three-way claim (USDC + Venmo/Cash App + NGN bank) was **not** on Tremendous's monetary table, Snack Money's API, Coinbase's blog, Peanut's help, or Request Finance's marketing page. — [Tremendous help](https://help.tremendous.com/hc/en-us/articles/41472317536787-Monetary-reward-options); [Peanut](https://peanut.me/en/help/send-money-link); [Request Finance](https://www.requestfinance.com/use-cases/mass-payouts)
- Peanut African bank claims are still "coming soon" on the older receive docs. — [Peanut receive](https://docs.peanut.to/how-to-receive-money-via-peanut)
- Africa send-to-non-users that *were* checked (Chipper, Flutterwave Send) require bank or momo at send time. — [Chipper](https://support.chippercash.com/en/articles/6282268-how-to-send-money-directly-to-non-chipper-recipients-bank-or-mobile-money-account-in-other-african-countries)

### Inferences
- Be honest with the founder: **thousands of Nigerian off-ramps do not mean the claim-link layer is empty.** They mean the *last-mile NGN rail* is commoditised. Loofta should not pitch "we invented pay-before-account."
- The remaining slice is **claim-side routing + identity**, closer to "Tremendous for stablecoin and African rails, triggered by an @handle" than to "TipLink." TipLink is a bearer Solana envelope. Tremendous is recipient choice without crypto/NGN. Coinbase is USDC-into-Coinbase. Snack Money is USDC-to-socials. Peanut/Morse are consumer claim-to-bank with thin or join-first Africa.
- That slice is **open but fragile**. Any of those four incumbents adding one missing rail closes most of the gap. Distribution and compliance (business KYB carrying recipient-light claims, then BVN at NGN cash-out) matter more than the escrow primitive.
- Consumer P2P "pay my friend who isn't on the app" is a terrible beachhead: every super-app already has it. The only wedge that matches Loofta's stated target is **business bulk to people the payer cannot KYC in advance** (creators, affiliates, social rewards, African contractors known only by handle/email).

### Gaps
- No market-size figure for "businesses that want to pay pending social handles" vs "businesses that already have contractor emails and bank details."
- Grey, Paystack Transfers, Fincra, Bitnob, Eversend not searched for email-claim products in this pass.
- Colosseum project lists were not enumerated (PAT unset).
