Companion to `CLIPPING_CREATOR_PLATFORM_PROSPECTS.md` (2026-09-26, clipping-only). This version adds UGC marketplaces and affiliate/commission SaaS, and prioritizes US-operated platforms.

**Everything here is public web info. Emails are only ones the company publishes itself. No contact details are invented. Verify the founder/contact on X or LinkedIn before sending, and re-check live campaigns before quoting ticket sizes.**

Legend: *(stated)* = the platform publishes the payout method; *(unknown)* = not found in public pages, ask.

## Sending notes (cold B2B outreach)

- One-to-one, relevant B2B cold email is generally allowed under CAN-SPAM (US) and PECR (UK/EU) if it's business-to-business and relevant to the role — but keep it human: no deceptive subject lines, include a real postal identity or a clear "reply STOP/unsubscribe" line, and honor unsubscribes immediately. Do not scrape private inboxes.
- Lead with the specific pain you verified (e.g. "your help center says PayPal or crypto"), not the product. One ask: "want to run one real payout file with us?"
- Send from a warmed domain, small batches, and track who replies. A reply is the only conversion that matters at this stage.
