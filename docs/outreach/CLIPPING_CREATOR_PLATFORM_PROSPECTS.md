## Suggested order

1. Reellu, Clippio, Onclip, P2P KOLs, Creator Wire, ClipStake. Small teams, one-message reach, and the pitch is concrete.
2. ClipAffiliates, Clipping.net, Promote.fun. Payout friction is documented publicly, so open with that.
3. Agencies (Lumina, Clipping Agency, Cliptic). Highest volume, pitch as "how do you pay your clippers today".
4. Kaito Studio, Whop, Vyro last. Long shots.
